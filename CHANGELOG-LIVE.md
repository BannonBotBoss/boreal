- 2026-10-15 08:45 PT: fix: tree wind sway works again in batches. - 2026-10-15 07:47 PT: startup loading screen and resource probe (ui/loading, render/startup, core/probe; detect-gpu 5.0.70 MIT vendored in vendor/detect-gpu with its benchmark files in public/detect-gpu). Behind the loading screen, in order: first chunks, renderer.compileAsync, sky/PMREM bake, then the probe, then a fade, so the startup stutter is hidden. The probe records the unmasked renderer, detect-gpu tier, MAX_TEXTURE_SIZE, deviceMemory and hardwareConcurrency, and benchmarks the real start scene at Ultra after shader warm-up (median of max(CPU work, GPU timer) over 40 frames). It derives GOVERNOR.probeCap, which both the governor and manual tiers stay at or below, using the cheapest signal; a measured bench overrides detect-gpu, because its database ranks the RTX 5060 Laptop as tier 1. It flags an Intel/AMD iGPU and caches per renderer string (?reprobe forces a new probe). The cap rises one tier after 3 sustained-headroom governor windows and is saved. Loading videos are remote https URLs listed in public/loading/manifest.json (none listed yet), with preload=metadata and a 3 s skip; with no clips or offline it shows boreal ecology cards. Omen: RTX 5060 Laptop (not the iGPU), bench 8.0 ms at Ultra, cap Ultra. Run-time cost: none after the fade; the probe takes about 1 s once per GPU/driver.

- 2026-10-15 08:05 PT: core/worldClock + core/stateFields + core/lifespan. One world clock that always ticks (monotonic performance.now plus a persisted epoch). The offline gap counts only when the wall clock moved forwards and is capped at 3 days, so changing the system clock cannot rewind or fast-forward the world. A single registry tags every state field 'clock' (age, growth, scale, time of day, season, weather, ground/delta ageing) or 'snapshot' (position, pose, motion, actions, and all vitals). A test fails on any untagged field of Entity, AnimalBrain, wolfModes or player. On pause, snapshot fields are captured and restored exactly (no catch-up), while clock fields keep following world time, including time of day. Player age is a clock field; the pause screen warns at 90% or more of the species' wild maximum (wolf 13, deer 20, bear 30, hare 5 y [verify]).
