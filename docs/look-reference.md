# Look reference

This is the board for the look fixes. The photos and footage are for study only. Do not put them, or any AI art, into the game or a demo video. Matthew's own picture is concept art for mood only.

A change counts as done when a recording from a real computer matches the line under each heading.

## Mood

What to match in the game: The mother should look grizzled and grey-brown, the pups fluffy and tabby-brown, and the forest light should feel dark and moody, like Matthew's picture. Use his picture for mood only — it is concept art, not a photo, and it should not go in the game or a demo video.

## The fox's trot

What to match in the game: The fox should trot, not slide. Opposite legs move together, the body stays level, and the paws lift and plant in time with how fast it is going.

## Pups around mama

What to match in the game: Pups should bunch in loose, messy little groups around their mother: tumbling, sniffing, bumping, and wandering a few steps away. They must never line up like soldiers.

## Shadows on the ground

What to match in the game: Every animal and every clump of grass should make a soft dark patch on the ground, especially under the feet, so nothing looks like it is floating. In bright sun the shadow can stretch. In a cloudy forest it should be faint and close. These photos are lighting references — match the shadows, not the species.

Study, not for the game:

## The ground under their feet

What to match in the game: The ground should always stay under the animals. When the land changes, keep the old ground on screen until the new one is ready, then swap. Forest ground is moss, needles, lichen, and fallen bits. Dry grassland is tan soil and grass stems, never a blank hole.

## Forest into dry grass

What to match in the game: The change from green forest or meadow into dry tan grass should be a slow, soft blend, like real parkland — not a hard line and not a blocky wave.

## Grass clumps

What to match in the game: Grass should look like real clumps: full, with mixed greens and tans, and a messy base where the stems meet the soil. It should not look like flat paper turning to face you.

## Where the words should sit

What to match in the game: Buttons and names should sit beside or just above the animals, with a little space, so you can still see their whole bodies. They should never cover the pups or the mother.
