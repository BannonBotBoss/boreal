# Boreal look reference

Photos and footage are for study only. Never put them, or any AI art, into the game.

## Mood

What to match: mama grizzled grey-brown, pups fluffy tabby-brown, forest light dark and moody.

Study only:

- https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Grey_wolves_in_Bavarian_Forest_National_Park_(cropped).jpg
- https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Grey_Wolf_Portrait.jpg

## 1. Ground

What to match: ground always stays under the animals; keep the old ground until the new one is ready, then swap. Forest floor is moss, needles, lichen and fallen bits; dry grassland is tan soil and grass stems, never a hole.

Study only:

- https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Moss_lichen_and_other_vegetation_cover_the_boreal_forests_floor.jpg
- https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Ergaki,_Forest_floor_in_taiga_forest,_Siberia,_Sayan_Mountains,_Russia.jpg

## 2. Fox

What to match: trots, never slides. Diagonal legs move together, body stays level, paws lift and plant in time with ground speed.

Study only:

- https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Fox_Trot_(50446799762).jpg

## 3. Pups

What to match: loose messy little groups around mama, tumbling, sniffing, bumping, wandering a few steps away; never lined up.

Study only:

- https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Wolf_Cubs_Playing_(42734897815).jpg

## 4. Shadows

What to match: every animal and grass clump makes a soft dark patch on the ground, especially under the feet. In bright sun shadows stretch; in cloudy forest they're faint and close.

Study only:

- https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Majestic_walk_(28983750418).jpg
- https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Cows_@_Plateau_du_Semnoz_(35471152596).jpg

## 5. Biome blend

What to match: green forest or meadow into dry tan grass is a slow soft blend like real aspen parkland, not a hard line or blocky wave.

Study only:

- https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Aspen_forest_and_grassland_in_Alberta,_Canada.jpg
- https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Grazed_Aspen_Parkland_-_panoramio.jpg

## 6. Grass

What to match: real full clumps, mixed greens and tans, messy base where stems meet soil; not flat cards turning to face you.

Study only:

- https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Festuca_hallii_at_Glenbow_Ranch_PP_Alberta.jpg
- https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Deschampsia_cespitosa_-_tufted_hairgrass_-_52512311891.jpg

## 7. Labels

What to match: buttons and names sit beside or just above animals with a little space, never covering the pups or mama.
