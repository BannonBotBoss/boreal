The script library

Everything visible is generated geometry. No stored 3D model files.

buildScene.ts assembles the view. geometry.ts builds shapes. world.ts generates temporary individuals. events.ts remembers interactions.

Move near the deer or the visitor and choose speak. This is a fictional capability demonstration, not a claim about animal language or a specific culture. Gathering the orange plant records its removal. Face a stone of 22 cm or less and bring your snout close: A / E / click activates move. Six contact sectors determine its rolling direction; the new position is remembered. Stones range from 6 to 50 cm, with smaller sizes more common.

Xbox: left stick left/right steers the wolf and up/down moves forward/backward; camera follows behind. Right stick looks around and tilts, RT runs, LT enters stealth; RT then LT sprints for 1.5 seconds; LT then RT enters super stealth with target focus. Release both to choose a new combination, A interact, Menu pause, B close this panel. Keyboard: W/S forward/backward, A/D steer, left/right arrows look around, up/down arrows tilt, Shift runs, C enters stealth; combine them in the same order as triggers, E interact, Escape pause, P photo mode (free camera, rewind, capture). Mouse: hold left or right button on the landscape and drag horizontally to orbit or vertically to tilt; manual zoom is disabled. Click the action button to interact. Tab and Enter operate menus. This is a terrain and interaction laboratory; collision, hunting, trading and construction are not implemented.

Unvisited chunks are not held in memory. Only interacted-with individuals are saved. The terrain is an illustrative river basin, not a historical map or full hydrology simulation.
