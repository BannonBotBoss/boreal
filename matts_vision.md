this game is supposed to be a digital twin of planet earth and its ecosystems and the player can embody any creature upon it.  everything is procederally generated, and the aim is to achieve 95% realism.  the behaviours must be based off real scientific data, and the animations must be based off realistic video examples using frame by frame comparison.  every single element of the game will be generated as a result of time multiplied by a complex set of algorithms, designed to be lightweight, following a set of rules based on transactional logic.  an animals code can be inspected and would reveal that a certain amount of time combined with a series of transactions between objects, converting values into state changes, and evolving an organized system of dynamic objects into the world you see.   players start off with a view of the world, and have a tool to control the value of time.  reversing it, will render a globe backwards however long they wish.  until there isnt even a globe.  or forward, until the suns plasma has stripped the planet.  or they can zoom just back to the begining of life, and navigate a watery world as a microscopic entity, chasing chemical nutrients and avoiding harsh environments.  but, if they just enter into the world now, they will see all kinds of life forms, living their lives, and doing so with remarkable accuracy to what you would find in nature.  use a tool to locate the largest of mammals, a blue whale.  watch it feed on plankton, raising a calf and breeching the surface for air.  then, if your interested, you may embody the whale.  with a choice of first person or third person perspectives, you can swim around and explore the deep sea. soon however, an urge to replenish air will be obvious, and a reward for swallowing a mouthful of small fish will be your priority.  all the while, the offspring that continue your species, your calf, watches and learns from your actions.  then, take flight from the bird eye view, and follow a bear around the forest, working together to locate food. this game is going to be an engaging simulation and educational experience.
