# Skill: rain shadow and the sea's reach inland

When to use: the ocean's seasonal anomaly dying out with distance from the ocean coast, and extra rain where the wind climbs a ridge. One planet grid, read through `weatherAtLatLon`. T-0085, issue #100. Issue #99 is the seasonal-habitat context; this skill does not score habitat, vegetation seasons, or migration.

## Steps
1. Read the weather with `weatherAtLatLon` in `src/core/planetWeather.ts`. The forest player is that query. Do not add a second climate function, a private grid, or a wasm plane. `LAYOUT_VERSION` stays where T-0082 left it. `climateRaw` answers only when the wasm is absent.
2. Coast distance is a cache on `Grid`, not a plane. `refresh_coast` in `rust/boreal-sim/src/grid.rs` runs a 4-connected Dijkstra. A cell with `LAND` below 0.5 is ocean and its distance is 0. Steps are haversine metres with `EARTH_RADIUS`. An FNV stamp of the binary mask skips the walk when the mask has not changed. Call it from `initialize`, `boot`, and `update_energy`.
3. The host writes the mask. `landSampleFromSurface` in `planetWeather.ts` keeps an inland lake as land even when the bed is below sea level (`kind === 'lake'`). Its elevation is the water surface, `max(0, level, h)`. Only `kind === 'ocean'`, or a non-lake sample at or below sea level, is water, and that elevation is 0. A frozen lake is not an ocean source. `planetLandSample` and `globeLandSample` both use that function.
4. The decay length is `ocean_decay_length_m` in `atmosphere/wind.rs`. Steady advection and relaxation still give `exp(−x/L)` (Hartmann 1994, ch. 11). The clock for the air the surface feels is the boundary layer: `τ = h / (C_H U)`, so `L = h / C_H`. `h` is `ABL_DEPTH_M` = 1000 m, the typical daytime mixed layer (Stull 1988). `C_H = κ² / ln(z_m/z0)²` is the neutral bulk coefficient already in `surface_balance` (`ra = ln(10/z0)² / (κ² U)`, FAO-56 eq. 4). `z_m` is `BULK_WIND_HEIGHT_M` = 10 m, the same height as `WIND_REF_M`. `z0` is `FOREST_Z0` = 1.5 m (Raupach 1994), because seeded land holds half a bucket and `land_kind` then selects forest. `κ` is 0.41 (Högström 1996). `U` cancels, including at the equator. Held & Suarez 1994 `τ` stays in `wind.rs` for the free atmosphere. It is not this length: at 50°N it made `L` about 5400 km, and no land in the Kenora latitude band is that far from the ocean, so every land cell stayed marine.
5. `maritime_weight` and `maritime_air` live in `climate_seed.rs`. Weight is 1 on water and `exp(−coast_m / L)` on land. `seed_cell` blends sea air and land air with that weight, then the existing bucket, snow, and humidity. `update_energy` writes the same blend, then the existing ICAO lapse on land. `climate_seed` must not import `wind.rs`. Pass `L` in.
6. The forest boot is `FOREST_NLAT` = 180 rows and twice that in longitude, one degree. The playable landmass is about a hundred kilometres across. A four-degree cell is wider than that, so its centre fell in the ocean and the bilinear read was mostly sea. A one-degree centre on that landmass is land. Globe tiers stay on `LAYER_TIERS`. Do not add a second forest query.
7. Rain shadow is `hydrosphere/orographic.rs`, called from `update_moisture` before Manabe `condense()`. Roe 2005 (Ann. Rev. Earth Planet. Sci. 33:645, eq. 6; Sawyer 1956, Smith 1979): `S = ρ q (u·∇z) exp(−z/H_m)` when the wind climbs, else 0. `H_m` is the existing `VAPOUR_SCALE_HEIGHT` (2000 m). Use the cell's specific humidity, not `q_sat`, so a dry column cannot rain the saturated upper bound. Cap the dump by the column's water, remove it from `q`, and add it to rain before the bucket and snow split. Centred slopes match `wind.rs` (`cos` of latitude floored at 0.02). A zero time step adds nothing, so boot's `update_moisture(0)` stays dry of this term. Do not replace `precipitation.rs`.
8. Rebuild `public/wasm/boreal_sim.wasm` (`npm run build:wasm`, target `wasm32-unknown-unknown`) and commit it. The TypeScript tests load that file.
9. Gates: `cargo test --manifest-path rust/boreal-sim/Cargo.toml --lib` and `npx tsx --test tests/rain-shadow-ocean.test.ts`. The ridge test asserts windward rain greater than leeward rain through `weatherAtLatLon`. The seasonal test asserts the July-minus-January range grows from open water to the coast to inland. The Kenora test asserts January at the stand is below 0 °C, no warmer than the pre-sea-term stand (−6.36 °C), and snowing, and that July is a summer without snow. Same-pose frames go under `tasks/evidence/T-0085/` via `node tasks/evidence/T-0085/capture.mjs before|after|fixed`.

- `L = h / C_H` is 21.4 km at the equator and at 50°N. The old Held & Suarez surface time is still in the file (`k_a = 1/40 day⁻¹`, `k_s = 1/4 day⁻¹`) and is not this length.
- Pure seed, July day 196 minus January day 15, 49.79°N, elevation 0: coast 3.25 K (equals the ocean column), half a fetch 20.67 K, one fetch 31.24 K, four fetches 46.72 K, full land column 47.53 K. Four fetches are past halfway from the ocean column to the land column.
- Environment and Climate Change Canada 1981–2010 daily averages: Tofino A, climate id 1038205, July 14.7 °C minus January 5.3 °C is 9.4 K. Kenora A, climate id 6034075, July 19.7 °C minus January −16.0 °C is 35.7 K. The game coast (3.25 K) is milder than Tofino. That comparison stays a no. `NORMALS` in `weather.ts` still says Kenora July 19.2 and was not edited.
- Relabeling inland lakes, and keeping only the largest ocean component, left the four-degree Kenora read at +2.28 °C in January. The warming was the long fetch plus ocean cells in the bilinear stencil, not Lake of the Woods. Lakes stay land anyway, so a punched lake bed cannot become a source on a finer grid.
- One 6 h Rust step, west wind 10 m/s, unsaturated so Manabe rain is 0: windward 0.635 mm/day, crest 0, leeward 0. Reversing the wind wets the other face.
- `weatherAtLatLon` after four 6 h steps on a 45°N crest, model westerlies: `u` 3.02 m/s, windward 2.425 mm/day, leeward 0.543 mm/day, ratio 4.46. Minder, Durran, Roe and Anders 2008 (Q. J. R. Meteorol. Soc.) give Forks 3.0 m/yr and Sequim 0.4 m/yr, ratio 7.5, on the Olympics. Direction yes. The ratio is not a numeric pass.
- Same query near 42.5°N on a half-degree mask: water 2.88 K, first land cell 33.04 K, inland 38.26 K. Monotonic.
- Forest stand, one-degree grid, eight 6 h steps, `weatherAtLatLon` at the player: January −18.94 °C, snow falling, 28.67 mm/day, snow-water 86.0 mm. July 27.50 °C, no snow. January is below the pre-sea-term −6.36 °C and closer to Kenora's −16.0 °C than either −6.36 °C or the merged +2.28 °C. July 27.50 °C against 19.7 °C is a warm summer and is not a pass. `h` and `z0` were not fitted to the station.

## Mistakes to avoid
- A second weather path, including a private half-degree grid. PR #128 was that path. PR #129's `weatherAtLatLon` is the query.
- Issue #100's uncited example coefficients (a 35° span, ±15°, a humidity percentage). Do not fit `L`, `h`, `z0`, or the Olympic ratio to a station.
- Using the Held & Suarez free-atmosphere time as the surface fetch. That `τ` is real and stays in `wind.rs`. It is the wrong clock for air a kilometre deep over this planet's landmasses.
- Counting an inland lake as ocean. Big lakes freeze. `kind === 'lake'` is land even when the bed is below 0. The Kenora miss was not caused by Lake of the Woods; measure that before treating the mask as the whole fix.
- Leaving the forest grid at four degrees. The stand's landmass is narrower than that cell, so the bilinear corners sit in the ocean and the sea term warms a place that is land at one degree.
- Editing `NORMALS` or `climateRaw` so Kenora matches. Record the January and July misses.
- Importing `wind.rs` from `climate_seed.rs`. Compute `L` in the wind module and pass it in.
- Replacing Manabe condensation, or adding orographic rain on a zero time step.
- Calling a magnitude a pass when the published place is a different geometry. Label the miss.
- Treating an uncalibrated look at the forest frames as the 95% gate. The transect is the climate check. The Kenora frames show whether snow is falling. A frame score without a calibrated metric stays uncalibrated.
