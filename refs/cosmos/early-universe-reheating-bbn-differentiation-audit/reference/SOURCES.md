   - **Author**: `en:User:PAR`

   - **What it shows in nature**: The physical CIE 1931 $(x, y)$ chromaticity trajectory of an isotropic Planckian blackbody radiation bath: at $T \gg 30,000\text{ K}$ (`k_B T ≫ h\nu`), chromaticity converges to the Rayleigh–Jeans asymptote $(x, y) \to (0.240, 0.234)$ (blue-white), passes through white near `5,500–6,500 K`, cools to warm orange-amber `(0.437, 0.404)` (`sRGB R/B ≈ 2.86`) at Recombination (`T ≈ 2,936–3,000 K`, `t ≈ 380 kyr`), reddens past `1,000 K`, and fades to complete visible darkness (`0 cd/m²` photopic luminance) during the infrared Cosmic Dark Ages (`T ≈ 132 K`, $\lambda_{\text{max}} = 21.9\,\mu\text{m}$ at `50 Myr`) — never glowing bright (`brightness = 1.23`) neon purple-violet (`[0.32, 0.05, 0.78]`, `R/B = 0.41`, off the non-spectral purple boundary).
