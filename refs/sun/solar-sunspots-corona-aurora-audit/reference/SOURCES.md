# Nature Reference Sources — Solar Photosphere, Sunspots, EUV Corona & Orbital Aurora Audit (`T-0242`)

All reference images are authentic NASA Solar Dynamics Observatory (SDO) and NASA International Space Station (ISS) photographs in the **Public Domain** (17 U.S.C. § 105, U.S. Federal Government works), retrieved via Wikimedia Commons:

1. **`ref-0.jpg` — Solar Photosphere Continuum & Centre-to-Limb Darkening (`4500 Å`)**
   - **Commons title**: `File:SDO 20130714 000007 4096 4500 (AIA).jpg`
   - **Source / Author**: NASA / Solar Dynamics Observatory (SDO) AIA Science Team (`2013-07-14T00:00:07Z`)
   - **Licence**: Public domain (NASA)
   - **URL**: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:SDO_20130714_000007_4096_4500_(AIA).jpg
   - **Physical feature**: Continuous centre-to-limb photospheric darkening $I(\mu)/I(1) = \mu^{\alpha(\lambda)}$ ($\alpha_{550} \approx 0.508$, Neckel & Labs 1994; Hestroffer & Magnan 1998) with zero flat-white interior clipping across $\rho \in [0, 1]$, paired with active-cycle sunspot groups.

2. **`ref-1.jpg` — Solar Cycle 25 Bipolar Sunspot Group Umbra/Penumbra & Heliographic Tilt (HMI Continuum)**
   - **Commons title**: `File:Sun Emits X6 3 Flare on February 22, 2024 (SVS14535 - SDO 02222024 X6 HMI-Continuum 4k).webm` (1280 px still frame)
   - **Source / Author**: NASA Goddard Space Flight Center Scientific Visualization Studio / SDO HMI (`2024-02-22`, AR 13590)
   - **Licence**: Public domain (NASA SVS #14535)
   - **URL**: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Sun_Emits_X6_3_Flare_on_February_22,_2024_(SVS14535_-_SDO_02222024_X6_HMI-Continuum_4k).webm
   - **Physical feature**: Warm reddish-brown $3900\,\text{K}$ umbrae ($B_\lambda(3900)/B_\lambda(5772)$ is $2.12\times$ brighter at $610\,\text{nm}$ than at $465\,\text{nm}$, Solanki 2003) surrounded by structured $5450\,\text{K}$ penumbrae along heliographically tilted active latitudes ($B_0 \in [-7.25^\circ, +7.25^\circ]$, Joy's law).

3. **`ref-2.png` — Optically Thin EUV `171 Å` (Fe IX `0.63 MK`) Corona & Dipole Magnetic Loops**
   - **Commons title**: `File:Sun Releases X3 3 Flare on February 9, 2024 (SVS14479 - SDO 2-14-2024 1302UTC 171).png`
   - **Source / Author**: NASA Goddard Space Flight Center Scientific Visualization Studio / SDO AIA `171 Å` (`2024-02-14T13:02Z`)
   - **Licence**: Public domain (NASA SVS #14479)
   - **URL**: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Sun_Releases_X3_3_Flare_on_February_9,_2024_(SVS14479_-_SDO_2-14-2024_1302UTC_171).png
   - **Physical feature**: Tone-mapped golden Fe IX `171 Å` corona with smooth arched magnetic dipole loops above bipolar active regions, unsaturated limb brightening, and extended off-limb hydrostatic emission ($H \approx 32\text{–}50\,\text{Mm}$, Lemen et al. 2012).

4. **`ref-3.jpg` — Orbital Nightside Auroral Oval & Off-Limb `110–300 km` Curtain Above `95 km` Airglow**
   - **Commons title**: `File:Aurora Australis From ISS edit1.JPG`
   - **Source / Author**: NASA Johnson Space Center, ISS Crew Earth Observations (`ISS023-E-58455`, 2010-05-29)
   - **Licence**: Public domain (NASA)
   - **URL**: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Aurora_Australis_From_ISS_edit1.JPG
   - **Physical feature**: Continuous auroral curtain rising across the dark nightside planetary disk and extending high above the $95\,\text{km}$ airglow horizon through the $110\,\text{km}$ green $\text{O}(^1\text{S})\ 557.7\,\text{nm}$ emission peak and $200\text{–}300\,\text{km}$ upper thermosphere without any $100\,\text{km}$ geometric cutoff (Chamberlain 1961; Feldstein & Starkov 1967).
