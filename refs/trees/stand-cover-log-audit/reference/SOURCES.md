# Visual Reference Sources (`T-0237`)

All reference images in this directory are unmanipulated Creative Commons / Public Domain field photographs from Wikimedia Commons:

1. **`ref-0-boreal-lake-ontario-shoreline.jpg`**
   - **File**: `File:Thunder-Lake-Ontario_715523.jpg` (`https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Thunder-Lake-Ontario_715523.jpg`)
   - **Location**: Thunder Lake, Aaron Provincial Park, Northwestern Ontario, Canada (~105 km east of Kenora, same Ecoregion 4S / Boreal Shield).
   - **Ground truth**: Open lacustrine water is completely free of submerged terrestrial black spruce, jack pine, or paper birch; littoral reeds occupy shallow marsh margins while boreal conifers and paper birch root strictly above the dry shoreline (`isDryLand`).

2. **`ref-1-sahara-erg-barren-dunes.jpg`**
   - **File**: `File:Sand_dune_in_the_desert,_Sahara_Desert,_Egypt.jpg` (`https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Sand_dune_in_the_desert,_Sahara_Desert,_Egypt.jpg`)
   - **Location**: Libyan / Sahara Desert, Egypt (Köppen `BWh` hyper-arid desert).
   - **Ground truth**: Wind-rippled quartz sand dunes with zero boreal spruce/birch trees, zero fallen timber, zero *Pleurozium*/*Sphagnum* moss, and zero *Carex*/*Calamagrostis*/*Vaccinium* ground flora.

3. **`ref-2-greenland-ice-sheet-barren.jpg`**
   - **File**: `File:The_surface_of_the_Greenland_Ice_Sheet.jpg` (`https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:The_surface_of_the_Greenland_Ice_Sheet.jpg`)
   - **Location**: Interior Greenland Ice Sheet (Köppen `EF` permanent continental ice).
   - **Ground truth**: Windswept snow and glacial ice sastrugi to the horizon with zero trees, zero fallen logs, and zero vascular graminoid or ericaceous shrub blades.

4. **`ref-3-boreal-nurse-log-moss-floor.jpg`** & **`ref-4-boreal-fallen-nurse-log-decay.jpg`**
   - **Files**:
     - `File:Swedish_Spruce_Forest.jpg` (`https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Swedish_Spruce_Forest.jpg`)
     - `File:Seedling_and_moss_on_nurse_log,_Berge_naturreservat_omkullfallet_liten_tall_(cropped).jpg` (`https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Seedling_and_moss_on_nurse_log,_Berge_naturreservat_omkullfallet_liten_tall_(cropped).jpg`)
   - **Ground truth**: Mature boreal spruce boles measure `15–28 cm` DBH (`L/D >= 30`); fallen downed wood matches parent stand diameter (never `64–120 cm` coastal-giant cylinders), fractures with jagged splintered ends rather than flat chainsaw-cut discs, excludes ground herbs along its true rotated axis, and slumps into mossy elliptical mounds (`H/W <= 0.45`) in Decay Classes 4–5.
