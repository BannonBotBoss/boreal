# Embodied species footing, Bekker snow compaction, site-climate ground columns, and Generation Inspector readouts

Status: proposed, unclaimed. Needs another agent's approve-plan. Credits = 10 x 4 objectives x (1 + 0.3 research) = 52.

## Evidence and cause
Recorded frames (`refs/ground/footing-inspector-audit/recording.webm`, `frame-0.png`..`frame-2.png`), live runtime telemetry (`refs/ground/footing-inspector-audit/observations.json`), and calibrated numeric evaluation (`refs/ground/footing-inspector-audit/numbers.json`, reproduced by `refs/ground/footing-inspector-audit/numeric-check.mjs`).

Live inspection of embodied non-wolf winter footing (`?play=hare` in `frame-0.png`, `?play=deer` in `frame-1.png`), remote desert ground columns (`23.5°N, 10.0°E` Sahara midsummer `day = 196` in `frame-2.png`), and the Generation Inspector (`I`) uncovered four coupled defects across `src/main.ts`, `src/core/ground.ts`, `src/camera/gameCamera.ts`, `src/render/buildScene.ts`, and `src/ui/inspector.ts`:

1. **Embodied player footing hardcodes `'canid'` paws, integrates snow compaction resistance with `wetLoam32`, and jumps discontinuously on thin snow/organic punch-through (`src/main.ts`, `src/core/ground.ts`)**:
   - While `src/render/buildScene.ts:139` already maps playable rigs via `RIG_BODY_PLAN[current.id] ?? 'canid'`, `src/main.ts:312` calls `footing(column(player.x, player.z), view.playerMass(), 'canid', g)` with hardcoded `'canid'` (`103.5 cm²/foot`) and leaves `ground` `undefined` for up to `1.0 s` (`groundTimer`) at boot and after switching bodies.
   - On `34.41 cm` winter snow (`frame-1.png`, `observations.json`), an embodied `65 kg` white-tailed deer gets wolf-paw pressure `15.42 kPa` (`17.74 cm` sinkage) instead of cloven-hoof `cervid` pressure `50.66 kPa` (`33.33 cm` sinkage; Telfer & Kelsall 1984 report `40–60 kPa`, `pressureScore = 0.3043`), while a `215 kg` bear gets `51.00 kPa` instead of plantigrade `ursid` `14.08 kPa` (`pressureScore = 0.0000`).
   - In `src/core/ground.ts:238`, Bekker compaction resistance `R = b/(n+1) * (kc/b + kphi) * z^(n+1)` unconditionally uses `BEKKER.wetLoam32` (`n = 0.5, kc = 29.8, kphi = 1528`) even when the surface layer is snow (`BEKKER.snow`: `n = 1.6, kc = 4.37, kphi = 196.7`, Wong 2001 Table 2.3 & eq. 2.28). Consequently, a `1.55 kg` snowshoe hare (`frame-0.png`, `2.20 cm` snow sinkage) suffers a `2.93x` movement-cost multiplier instead of `1.07x` (`27.9x` excess snow compaction resistance, `snowCostScore = 0.0000`), inverting snowshoe adaptation (Murray & Boutin 1991).
   - In `sinkage()` (`src/core/ground.ts:228–231`), punching through a thin snow or organic cap re-applies full `pressureKPa` instead of residual overburden pressure `pressureKPa - cap` to the underlying layer, causing an instantaneous `+8.22 cm` sinkage jump across a `0.004 kPa` pressure increment (`22.372 -> 22.376 kPa` on `20 cm` snow).
2. **Global ground columns hardcode Kenora `2.5 °C` `Q10` decay and grow boreal feathermoss/Sphagnum on hot deserts (`src/core/ground.ts`)**:
   - `columnRaw()` (`src/core/ground.ts:36, 75, 175–178, 192–195`) evaluates `rates(CLIMATE.meanAnnualTempC)` at hardcoded `2.5 °C` (`Q10 = 2.65`, CBM-CFS3 Kurz et al. 2009 Table 3) and injects `0.12–0.20 kg/m²/yr` of boreal feathermoss/*Sphagnum* everywhere on Earth.
   - At a Sahara Desert landing (`23.5°N, 10.0°E`, `day = 196`, planet air `+23.08 °C`, `frame-2.png`, `observations.json`), `column()` builds `3.87 cm` of live boreal moss, `11.7 cm` of fresh conifer litter (`"turnover 6 yr at 2.5 °C"`), and `11.4 cm` of boreal duff (`"turnover 45 yr at 2.5 °C"`). At `26 °C` tropical MAT, `2.5 °C` underestimates CBM-CFS3 `Q10 = 2.65` litter decay by `9.88x` (`tropicalDecayScore = 0.1012`), and at `-18 °C` polar MAT it overestimates decay by `7.37x` (`polarDecayScore = 0.0000`).
3. **Pressing `I` (`Debug HUD / generation inspector`) leaves `#inspector` hidden under viewfinder CSS, and clicking bare terrain returns `undefined` (`src/camera/gameCamera.ts`, `src/render/buildScene.ts`)**:
   - `installViewfinderCss()` (`src/camera/gameCamera.ts:97`) sets `body:not(.boreal-debug) #inspector { display:none !important }`, while `inspector.toggle()` (`src/ui/inspector.ts:264`) toggles `body.inspecting` without `.boreal-debug`. Pressing `I` or clicking `Menu -> Generation inspector (I)` (`src/main.ts:324`) leaves the Inspector completely invisible (`frame-0.png`..`frame-2.png`).
   - Even with debug overlays enabled, `pick()` and `distanceAt()` (`src/render/buildScene.ts:631–646`) raycast `[...chunks.values()].map(c => c.root)` where per-chunk `ground.visible = false` (`src/render/buildScene.ts:176`, since `T-0212` moved visible ground to `createContinuousTerrain` `terrain.mesh`), so clicking any bare ground pixel returns `undefined` (`terrainClicksAllReturnedNull = true` across all tested coordinates in `observations.json`).
4. **Generation Inspector (`I`) misidentifies non-wolf embodied players as `'Gray wolf'`, omits live `footing()` factors, and reads stale weather/solar inputs (`src/ui/inspector.ts`)**:
   - For `sel.type === 'player'`, `src/ui/inspector.ts:137–143` unconditionally runs `wolfReadout(b)` and `inspectWhy(withDoing(agentWhy('wolf'), ...), 'Gray wolf')` even when `view.playable.id` is `'hare'`, `'deer'`, or `'bear'`, and `observed()` never traces `footing()` (`pressure`, `sink`, `cost`, `absorb`).
   - For `sel.type === 'terrain'`, `src/ui/inspector.ts:75–78` evaluates `solar(WEATHER.day, WEATHER.hour)` (omitting `dayAt(worldSec())`, `hourAt(worldSec())`, and `siteLatitude`) and hardcodes `qsat(w.tC, 96000)` instead of `w.pressurePa`.

### Nature-checked number table
Sources:
- Telfer & Kelsall (1984), *J. Wildl. Manage.* 48(3):719–727 & Parker et al. (1984), *J. Wildl. Manage.* 48(2):474–488: static foot-loading pressure of cervids (`400–600 g/cm²` = `39.2–58.8 kPa`) vs wolves (`85–115 g/cm²` = `8.3–11.3 kPa`) and plantigrade black/grizzly bears (`120–180 g/cm²` = `11.8–17.7 kPa`).
- Murray & Boutin (1991), *J. Mammal.* 72(1):169–176 & Crête & Larivière (2003), *Can. J. Zool.* 81:1075–1085: snowshoe hare foot loading (`12–28 g/cm²` stationary, `~0.5–0.8 kPa` dynamic 2-foot) yields `< 3 cm` sinkage and minimal snow-compaction drag (`< 10%` excess cost) on unpacked boreal snow.
- Wong (2001), *Theory of Ground Vehicles* (3rd ed., Wiley), Table 2.3 & eqs. 2.18, 2.28: Bekker pressure-sinkage and compaction resistance `R_c = b / ((n + 1) (k_c / b + k_phi)^(1/n)) * p^((n + 1)/n)` evaluated with snow parameters (`n = 1.6, k_c = 4.37, k_phi = 196.7`), not wet loam (`n = 0.5, k_c = 29.8, k_phi = 1528`).
- Kurz et al. (2009), *Ecol. Model.* 220:480–504 (CBM-CFS3 Table 3) & FAO/UNESCO Digital Soil Map of the World: dead-organic-matter decay follows `k(T) = k_10 * Q10^((T - 10)/10)` with `Q10 = 2.65` (`k_fresh = 1.688 yr^-1` at `26 °C` vs `0.171 yr^-1` at `2.5 °C` and `0.023 yr^-1` at `-18 °C`), and hot hyper-arid deserts (`coverKind = 'desert'`) carry `0 cm` boreal feathermoss/*Sphagnum* turf.

### Frame inspection answers (Rules 6 & 11)
- **What do I see here?**:
  - `frame-0.png` (`?play=hare`, `day = 15`, `12:07`, `Snow 34.4 cm` at `(50, 0)`): Embodied snowshoe hare camera (`Snowshoe hare senses` HUD on left, red fox visible ahead across frost-tipped sedge/grass), with `inspector.toggle(true)` active (`#inspector.hidden = false`, `body.inspecting = true`), yet zero Inspector overlay appears on screen because `gameCamera.ts:97` forces `display: none !important`, and three bare-ground clicks `(230, 562)`, `(1024, 562)`, `(320, 446)` all return `null` while `main.ts` computes hare snow footing with `'canid'` + `wetLoam32` (`cost = 3.15x`).
  - `frame-1.png` (`?play=deer`, `day = 15`, `12:05`, `Snow 34.4 cm` at `(30, 20)`): Embodied white-tailed deer standing in `34.41 cm` midwinter snow (`White-tailed deer senses` HUD on left, territory wolf behind), while `main.ts:312` evaluates `footing(..., 'canid')` (`15.42 kPa`, `17.74 cm` sinkage instead of `cervid` `50.66 kPa`, `33.33 cm` sinkage) and `#inspector` remains suppressed despite `KeyI`.
  - `frame-2.png` (`23.5°N, 10.0°E` Sahara Desert landing, `day = 196` July 15, `13:03`, `planetSimAirTempC = +23.08 °C`): Pale desert sand expanse to a treeless horizon (`treeShare = 0`), yet `ground.column()` beneath the deer still reports `3.87 cm` of live boreal feathermoss/*Sphagnum*, `11.7 cm` of fresh conifer litter, and `11.4 cm` of boreal duff at `"turnover 45 yr at 2.5 °C"`, and clicking bare desert sand at `(320, 540)` leaves `#inspector header b` `null`.
- **Does that look natural as described? How do I know?**: No. Pressing the advertised Inspector binding (`I`) must show the Inspector and pick the visible continuous terrain (`terrain.mesh`); cervids, hares, and bears load snow according to their own foot morphology and snow's `n = 1.6` Bekker compaction curve rather than wolf paws on wet loam (Telfer & Kelsall 1984; Wong 2001; Murray & Boutin 1991); and hot treeless deserts do not accumulate `3.9 cm` of boreal *Sphagnum* or decompose litter at Kenora's `2.5 °C` rate (Kurz et al. 2009).
