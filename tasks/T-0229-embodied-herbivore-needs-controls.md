## Evidence and cause
Recorded frames (`refs/animals/embodied-herbivore-audit/recording.webm`, `frame-0.png`..`frame-2.png`), live runtime telemetry (`refs/animals/embodied-herbivore-audit/observations.json`), and calibrated numeric evaluation (`refs/animals/embodied-herbivore-audit/numbers.json`, reproduced by `refs/animals/embodied-herbivore-audit/numeric-check.mjs`).

Live inspection of embodied non-wolf gameplay (`?play=hare` in `frame-0.png`, `?play=deer` in `frame-1.png`, and `Autonomous: On` on `?play=deer` in `frame-2.png` on midsummer `day = 196`, `hour = 12`) uncovered four coupled defects across `src/core/needs/species/index.ts`, `src/main.ts`, `src/render/playables.ts`, `src/core/events.ts`, `src/ui/senses.ts`, `src/core/needs/thoughts.ts`, `src/render/thoughtBubbles.ts`, and `src/audio/borealSound.ts`:

1. **White-tailed deer (`?play=deer`) has no `DEER_NEEDS` template in `NEEDS_SPECIES`, silently running on `40 kg` carnivore `WOLF_NEEDS` (`src/core/needs/species/index.ts`, `src/main.ts`, `src/render/playables.ts`)**:
   - `NEEDS_SPECIES` (`src/core/needs/species/index.ts:182`) only registers `{ wolf: WOLF_NEEDS, hare: HARE_NEEDS }`. Whenever `view.playable.id === 'deer'`, `needsSpecies()` (`src/main.ts:164`) and `createSensesHUD` (`src/ui/senses.ts:87`) fall back to `WOLF_NEEDS` (`40 kg` carnivore Gray Wolf; confirmed in `observations.json` Frame 1).
   - Consequently, an embodied adult white-tailed deer (`MAMMAL_PLANS.cervid.massKg = 70 kg`, Fuller 1990 / Nowak 1999) runs at `40 kg` mass (`massScore = 0.5714`), `15,034 kJ/d` FMR instead of `23,157.8 kJ/d` (Nagy, Girard & Brown 1999 *Annu. Rev. Nutr.* 19:247 Table 2 eq. 2; Worden & Pekins 1995 *Can. J. Zool.* 73:452; `fmrScore = 0.6492`), and `1,895 mL/d` water turnover instead of `2,995.8 mL/d` (Nagy & Peterson 1988 *UC Publ. Zool.* 120; Lautier et al. 1988 *J. Wildl. Manage.* 52:602; `waterScore = 0.6326`).
   - With `view.playable.speedScale = 1.15` (`src/render/playables.ts:33`) over `WOLF_NEEDS`, the embodied deer walks at `2.30 m/s` vs `DEER_AGENT.speed.approach = 1.30 m/s` (`walkSpeedScore = 0.2308`), runs at `6.90 m/s` vs Heglund & Taylor (1988, *J. Exp. Biol.* 138:301) preferred gallop `2.78 * 70^0.116 = 4.551 m/s` (`runSpeedScore = 0.4837`), sprints at `12.075 m/s` vs `DEER_AGENT.speed.flee = 15.646 m/s` (`35 mph`, Rue 1997; `sprintSpeedScore = 0.7717`), and drains sprint stamina in `12 s` instead of `DEER_AGENT` `45 s` (`staminaScore = 0.2667`).
   - In `frame-2.png`, enabling `Autonomous: On` (`#autonomous`) on `?play=deer` runs `WOLF_NEEDS` dog osmotic thirst (`water 45% < 85%`) and `autonomousWolfStep` (`WOLF_AGENT`).

2. **Embodied herbivores (`?play=hare`, `?play=deer`) transfer `0.00 kJ` (`+0.00%` Energy) on manual food actions, and `applyPending()` wipes saved/`?needs=` Energy on non-wolf boot (`src/main.ts`, `src/core/events.ts`, `src/render/playables.ts`)**:
   - Although `src/core/needs/diet.ts:5` states *"herbivores graze bite bouts (an embodied hare at a clover patch)"* and `src/render/playables.ts:43` states *"eating is 'gather'"*, clover patches (`cloverPatchesInTile` / `grazeTransaction` in `src/core/fields.ts`) and deer browse (`BROWSE.at` / `BROWSE.eat` in `src/core/species.ts:260`) are continuous fields rather than `catalog` entities. In `frame-1.png`, the embodied deer stands directly on `7,180.8 g` of fresh browse, yet `#act` stays disabled (`"Approach an individual"`) and pressing `E` changes Energy by `-0.0023%` (`manualFeedingScore = 0.0000`).
   - Approaching a `'plant'` entity as `?play=hare` and pressing `E` (`gather`, `src/core/events.ts:63–65`) only increments `context.inventory.herb++`, never calling `dietOf(needsSpecies()).eat(...)` (`+0.00%` Energy vs `0.4076 kJ` / `+0.0366%` Energy per `0.362 g` wet clover bite, Belovsky 1984 *Oecologia* 61:150). Meanwhile `'forage'` berry patches are explicitly blocked for players in `src/main.ts:175,178` (`action === 'forage'`).
   - Furthermore, `src/main.ts:161–163` parses `localStorage.getItem(NEEDS_KEY)` (`energy`) and `?needs=energy:8` *before* `applyPending()` runs `view.setPlayable(playAs)` at `src/main.ts:336`, which calls `mirrorStoredAnimal` and overwrites `view.playerBrain.energy` back to `74.91` on every non-wolf load (`frame-0.png`, `frame-1.png`; `bootEnergyPersistenceScore = 0.0000`).

3. **Senses HUD (`#senses`) is blind and anosmic to `wolf` and `fox` predators, hardcodes the `2,400 m` wolf nose on prey, and lacks `'head-sensor'` on the playable hare rig (`src/ui/senses.ts`, `src/render/playables.ts`)**:
   - In `src/ui/senses.ts:20–21, 104–119`, `potency` and `visualKind` only list `catalog` kinds (`deer`, `hare`, `person`, `forage`, `plant`, `log`) and iterate `view.entities()` + `view.hares.list()`, completely omitting `view.audibleAnimals()` NPC predators (`wolf`, `fox`). In `frame-0.png`, a Red Fox stands directly ahead of the embodied Snowshoe Hare, and in `frame-1.png`–`frame-2.png`, a Gray Wolf stands ~3 m behind the embodied White-tailed Deer, yet `#senses` displays zero sight or scent signals (`sensesPredatorDetectionScore = 0.0000`).
   - `src/ui/senses.ts:87` also gives `?play=deer` the wolf's `250°` binocular predator FOV and `60 m` sight range instead of white-tailed deer `310°` panoramic vision (D'Angelo et al. 2008, *J. Wildl. Manage.* 72:1738; `deerFovScore = 0.8065`) and `80 m` sight range (`DEER_AGENT`, `deerSightRangeScore = 0.7500`), while `src/ui/senses.ts:121` hardcodes `deltaSources(..., WOLF_AGENT.senses.scent.range)` (`2,400 m`, Mech 1970) even when embodied as `deer` (`DEER_AGENT` trace range `2.0 m`, van Ginkel et al. 2019 *PLoS ONE* 14:e0223248; `deerTraceRangeScore = 0.0000`).
   - In `src/render/playables.ts:38,47`, the playable `hare` and `bear` rigs create `sensor = new THREE.Object3D()` without `sensor.name = 'head-sensor'`, causing `thoughtBubbles.ts:34` and `senses.ts:90` (`getObjectByName('head-sensor') ?? wolf`) to fall back to root origin `y = 0`.

- **What do I see here?**:

- **Does that look natural as described? How do I know?**: No. White-tailed deer are `~70 kg` browsers with `310°` panoramic vision, `2 m` trace avoidance, and `~23.2 MJ/d` FMR rather than `40 kg` carnivores with `2,400 m` scent tracking (Nagy et al. 1999; D'Angelo et al. 2008; van Ginkel et al. 2019); wild snowshoe hares and deer feed continuously on clover, herbs, berries, and browse rather than starving on lush summer swards or hunting hares and howling like wolves (Belovsky 1984; Koerth & Stuth 1991); and prey Senses HUDs must detect nearby wolves and foxes.
