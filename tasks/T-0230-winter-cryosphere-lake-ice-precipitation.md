# Continuous-terrain snowpack and wetness, seasonal lake ice freeze-up, world-space wind-slanted precipitation, and active snowfall vs snowpack state

Status: proposed, unclaimed. Needs another agent's approve-plan. Credits = 10 x 4 objectives x (1 + 0.3 research) = 52.

## Plain-English summary
In mid-January (`-19 °C`, HUD reading `Snow 34.2 cm`), the ground and hills look bare brown-green like summer because the continuous terrain mesh never fills its snow or rain-wetness vertex attributes (`aSnow = 0`, `aWet = 0`), and the boreal lake stays open liquid blue-teal water with animated ripples instead of freezing over under ice and snow. Meanwhile, falling rain and snow are glued to the camera (`0 m` parallax when the wolf runs), barely tilt in strong wind (`7.6°` instead of `53°` in `8 m/s` wind), speed up `12x` in screen real time when the time dial speeds up, and weather catch-up confuses snow already on the ground (`sweMm > 0.5`) with active falling snow (`w.snow`), erasing wolf tracks `4x` too fast on clear winter days. This task wires live snowpack and wetness onto the continuous terrain, freezes lakes with ice and snow cover in sub-zero seasons, anchors and tilts precipitation in world space at real-time fall speeds, and separates active snowfall from ground snowpack.

## Evidence and cause
Recorded gameplay video (`refs/weather/winter-cryosphere-audit/recording.webm`), sampled frames (`frame-0.png`..`frame-2.png`), photorealistic nature references (`refs/weather/winter-cryosphere-audit/reference/ref-0-boreal-lake-january-snowpack.png`, `ref-1-boreal-rain-wind-streaks.png`), live runtime telemetry (`observations.json`), and calibrated Rule 11 numeric evaluation (`numbers.json`, reproduced by `npx tsx refs/weather/winter-cryosphere-audit/numeric-check.mjs`).

Live inspection of the `3.06 ha` home boreal lake and shoreline on January 15 (`day = 15`, `hour = 12`, `tC = -19.03 °C`, `Snow 34.2 cm` in `frame-0.png` and `frame-1.png`) and during a mid-August wind-driven rainstorm (`day = 227`, `hour = 14`, `tC = +23.9 °C`, `4.5 mm/h`, `8 m/s` wind in `frame-2.png`) uncovered four coupled defects across `src/render/continuousTerrain.ts`, `src/render/water.ts`, `src/render/sky.ts`, and `src/core/weather.ts`:

1. **`continuousTerrain.ts` leaves `aSnow` and `aWet` permanently zero across all `40,833` vertices (`src/render/continuousTerrain.ts:155–156, 265`)**:
   - Since `T-0212` hid per-chunk terrain plates (`ground.visible = false` in `src/render/buildScene.ts:175`), visible ground is drawn exclusively by `createContinuousTerrain()`.
   - `continuousMaterial()` clones `terrainMaterial()` (`src/render/pbr.ts:56–89`), whose shader already shades snow cover (`diffuseColor -> vec3(0.80)`, suppressing litter/moss cover by `1 - snow` and smoothing micro-normal roughness) from `aSnow` and rain-wet darkening/specular sheen (`diffuseColor *= 1 - 0.4 * vWet`, `roughness 0.95 -> 0.35`) from `aWet`.
   - However, `buildContinuousMesh()` (`src/render/continuousTerrain.ts:155–156`) allocates zeroed `wet = new Float32Array(n)` and `snow = new Float32Array(n)` and **never populates them**, and `update()` (`line 265`) omits snow/wet state from its rebuild cache key `next`.
   - On January 15 (`tC = -19.03 °C`, `sweMm = 85.60 mm`, `depthCm = 34.24 cm`, `frame-0.png`), `maxASnow = 0` across all `40,833` vertices (`349` quadtree leaves), leaving shoreline banks and background hills bare olive-brown (`albedo ~0.083` vs `0.80` dry snow; `score = 0.0000` / `0.1037`). In an August downpour (`frame-2.png`), `maxAWet = 0` (`score = 0.0000`).
2. **Boreal lakes remain open liquid `#578d91` water with animated Vlachos ripples and wake rings at `-19 °C` in January (`src/render/water.ts:23–65`)**:
   - Northwestern Ontario Shield lakes (`49.79°N`, Experimental Lakes Area / Kenora) freeze completely by mid-to-late November (`DOY ~320–330`) and remain `100%` ice- and snow-covered (`albedo 0.55–0.85`, zero free-surface gravity waves or ripples) until late April / early May (`DOY ~115–130`; Schindler et al. 1990 *Science* 250:967; Duguay et al. 2006 *Hydrol. Process.* 20:781).
   - `waterMaterial()` (`src/render/water.ts:25–55`) has zero freeze/ice or snow-on-ice transition: in `frame-0.png` and `frame-1.png` (`day = 15`, `-19.0 °C`, `sweMm = 85.6 mm`), the entire lake renders as open teal liquid water (`#578d91`, `roughness = 0.32`) with animated `wgrad` surface waves and expanding concentric liquid wake rings (`score = 0.0000`).
3. **Precipitation particles are camera-locked (`0 m` parallax), under-advected by wind (`0.1x` rain / `0.5x` snow), isotropic `Points` squares in rain, and driven by world-clock `dt` (`12x` runaway fall speed) (`src/render/sky.ts:125–129, 179, 188–195`)**:
   - `precip.position.copy(camera.position)` (`src/render/sky.ts:191`) rigidly translates the `40 m` particle box with the camera every frame; when the wolf runs `8.497 m` forward in `frame-1.png`, particles move `0.00 m` relative to the camera (`score = 0.0000`), eliminating forward optic-flow parallax.
   - Horizontal wind advection (`src/render/sky.ts:192`) multiplies `w.wind` by `0.5` for snow and `0.1` for rain, whereas raindrops (`v_t = 6.0 m/s`, relaxation time `tau = v_t / g = 0.61 s`, Gunn & Kinzer 1949) and snowflakes (`v_t = 1.0 m/s`, `tau = 0.10 s`, Locatelli & Hobbs 1974) equilibrate to `1.00x` horizontal wind within `< 2 m` of fall. In an `8.0 m/s` wind (`frame-2.png`), game rain tilts only `atan2(0.8, 6.0) = 7.59°` instead of `atan2(8.0, 6.0) = 53.13°` (`score = 0.4940`), and `THREE.PointsMaterial` (`size = 0.05`) draws isotropic squares instead of velocity-aligned rain streaks (`ref-1-boreal-rain-wind-streaks.png`).
   - `src/render/sky.ts:179` computes `dt = Math.min(0.2, time - lastTime)` from `worldSec` rather than real frame `dt`: turning the `#109` clock dial to `15x` (`60 FPS`) advances `y -= speed * 0.2` every frame, accelerating `1.0 m/s` snowflakes to `12.0 m/s` in screen real time (`score = 0.0833`).
4. **Ground snowpack (`sweMm > 0.5`) is conflated with active falling snow (`w.snow`), accelerating clear-day winter footprint decay `4x` and misclassifying bare-ground snowfall (`src/core/weather.ts:313, 483, 492, 529` & `src/core/deltas.ts:48`)**:
   - `climateSurface()` (`weather.ts:483`), `mixWeather()` (`492`), and `catchUpWeather()` (`529`) compute `snow = precipFallsAsSnow(tC) && sweMm > 0.5` without checking `precipMmH > 0`.
   - After `catchUpWeather(3600)` on a clear January day (`precip = 0 mm/h`, `sweMm = 85 mm`), `grid.snowfall` becomes `1` (`w.snow = true`), causing `deltas.ts:48` (`wet = ... w.snow ? 4 : 1`) to decay wolf footprints `4x` faster (`0.5 d` strength `0.2636` vs expected dry-cold `exp(-0.5 / 1.5) = 0.7165`, `score = 0.3679`; Rezendes 1999).
   - Conversely, during early-November sub-zero snowfall (`day = 315`, `tC = -5 °C`, `precip = 2.8 mm/h`) onto bare ground (`sweMm = 0`) in mixed `weatherAt()`, `sweMm > 0.5` evaluates to `false`, turning sub-zero snowfall into liquid `6 m/s` rain (`score = 0.0000`; Dai 2008). In addition, `weather.ts:313` wraps `WEATHER.day = WEATHER.day % 365 + 1` on midnight crossings while `dayAt(worldSec())` is unbounded across multi-year runs.

## Objectives

- [ ] Add temperature- and SWE-driven seasonal lake/pond ice and snow-over-ice shading to `src/render/water.ts` (`waterMaterial` + `updateWaterUniforms`): freeze low-flow/still water surfaces (`length(vFlow.xy)` below river-rapid threshold) when freezing conditions / winter ice cover apply (suppressing `wgrad` liquid ripples and `wakeField` rings on frozen sheets, raising diffuse albedo to `0.55–0.82` according to snow cover over ice, Schindler et al. 1990; Duguay et al. 2006) while keeping turbulent high-velocity river rapids partially open where physically appropriate.
