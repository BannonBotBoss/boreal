# Wolf litter ontogeny and seasonal cohort age, #109 clock-scale coupling, shoreline passability, and #309 Red Fox maternal-defense gating

## Plain-English summary
In Boreal, the mother wolf always spawns tiny four-week spring pups even in mid-January deep snow (`34 cm`), and then grows them `2,191x` faster than the world calendar (`4 hours` per full year) until five giant woolly pups (`1.21x` longer and `1.55x` bulkier than their mother) crowd right under her tail while the Red Fox still treats those grown wolves as `2.5 kg` snacks. Pups also ignore the `#109` time-scale dial: at `20x` Behavioral scale when wild animals fold into ground density patches (`simDt = 0`), pups stay fully drawn and keep walking and spinning, jump `0.45 m` in a single frame (`27 m/s`), and keep advancing during `-10x` rewind. Neither pups nor the Red Fox check staged lakes or rivers (`surfaceAt`), so starting on dry shore drops the fox `1.36 m` underwater and marches the mother and all five pups into open lake water. Worst of all, `stepDrives` runs even in fox-free biomes (`census = 0`), tows the lingering fox along with the running mother at `12.4 m/s`, and reels the fox right back every `8.43 s` (`420 charges/hr`), seizing the player's controls for `33%` of all gameplay time.
