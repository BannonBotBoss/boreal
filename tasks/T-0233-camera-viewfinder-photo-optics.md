# Camera terrain/water/obstacle clearance, Viewfinder EV100 photometry & lens/filter optics, Photo-mode thin-lens DoF & animal autofocus, and 60 s rewind completeness

Status: proposed, unclaimed. Needs another agent's approve-plan. Credits = 10 x 4 objectives x (1 + 0.3 research) = 52.

## Plain-English summary
In Boreal, the follow camera (`positionCamera`), the free-fly Photo/Drone camera (`photoFlyFloor`), the shared Viewfinder (`mountViewfinder`), and Photo Mode (`createPhotoMode` / `createPost`) each violate physical camera geometry and thin-lens / photometric optics:
1. **Camera clips into hillsides, plunges underwater beneath upland lakes, and sits inside tree trunks**: `positionCamera` checks ground height at the animal's paws rather than at the lens `1.28–4.40 m` behind it (dropping playable hare lens clearance to `0.081 m < 0.20 m` near-clip plane on banks), `photoFlyFloor` hardcodes the legacy `-0.25 m` water level so descending over a `17.631 m` staged upland lake plunges the aerial camera `1.055 m` underwater into a dry air void, and zero spring-arm raycast prevents tree trunks or boulders from swallowing the lens.
2. **Viewfinder exposure, focal-length slider, spectral filter stops, and photo timestamps are broken or dead**: `buildScene.ts` mounts `mountViewfinder` without `autoEV`, so the Camera panel reports `EV100 15.0` (`100,000 lux` noon sun) even at `00:06` astronomical midnight (`EV100 ≈ -6.97`). Worse, on High/Ultra tiers (`ao > 0`), Three.js `OutputPass` copies `renderer.toneMappingExposure` *before* `gameCamera.ts`'s `renderer.render` hook runs — making **every** manual exposure control (`Comp EV`, `Aperture f/`, `Shutter`, `ISO`, `ND stops`) a `0.000`-stop no-op — while on Low/Medium tiers (`ao = 0`), manual night settings (`f/1.4, 1 s, ISO 3200`) multiply exposure by `+19.03 stops` (`534,933.5x`) and blow midnight to pure white. Meanwhile, the Viewfinder `Focal mm` slider (`?focal=200` -> `6.867°` FOV) is completely ignored (`buildScene.ts` hardcodes `48°`), Red/Green/Blue filters report `0.000` stops of light loss instead of CIE photopic losses (`2.23 / 0.48 / 3.79` stops), and PNG `Boreal-Camera` metadata freezes `gameTime` at `hourAt(0)` (`12:00` even at `14:03`).
3. **Photo-Mode autofocus shoots straight through animals, and landscape `f/16` hyperfocal shots blur distant scenery to `25.6 px` mush**: `distanceAt` raycasts only chunk static roots — omitting `wolf.root`, `deer`, `hares`, `pups`, `landingCast`, and `continuousTerrain` — so clicking or center-autofocusing directly on the wolf `4.40 m` ahead focuses at `14.6–77.7 m` (`40.0 m` center autofocus) on background terrain. And `bokehAperture` omits focus distance `S1` while `BokehShader` uses unbounded linear depth `focus + viewZ`, clamping distant trees (`400 m`) at `50 mm, f/16, focus = 30 m` (hyperfocal `H = 5.21 m`, physical CoC `0.072 px`) to `maxblur = 0.02` (`25.60 px` blur radius).
4. **Photo-Mode's 60 s rewind slider leaves the sun/sky clock, wolf pups, landing cast, and visitors unrewound**: scrubbing back `−14.8 s` (`76` snapshots) rewinds the player wolf `2.133 m` while leaving `worldClock` / solar sky (`18.933 s` elapsed) and `pups` (`1.934 m` moved) stranded at live exit time (`5 / 9` dynamic subsystems registered).
