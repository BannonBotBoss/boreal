# Home Kenora census longitude (+65.5 E Kazakhstan bug), sideways GLB rigging & 2.22 m ground-walking Bald Eagle, landingCast chorus-line treadmilling/teleportation, and solid-rectangle mid-tier animal impostors

Status: proposed, unclaimed. Needs another agent's approve-plan. Credits = 10 x 4 objectives x (1 + 0.3 research) = 52.

## Plain-English summary
In Boreal, the wildlife landing census (`landingCast.ts` / `landingRoster.ts`), the stored GLB fitting and auto-skinning pipeline (`animalModels.ts` / `meshySkin.ts`), and the distance LOD ladder (`animalPicture.ts` / `proceduralAnimal.ts`) suffer from four severe, coupled defects:
1. **Default home launch thinks Kenora, Ontario is in Kazakhstan (`+65.54° E`), wiping out `11 / 12` North American wildlife species**: On normal home launch (`remote === false`), `landingContext` computes longitude from `planet.fromLocal(x, z)` -> `lat = 49.77° N, lon = +65.54° E` (Central Asia, with `coastM = NaN`) instead of Kenora, Ontario (`49.77° N, -94.36° W` in `weather.ts`). Because `species-density.json` restricts North American fauna (`Moose`, `Black Bear`, `Caribou`, `Canada Lynx`, `Beaver`, `Bald Eagle`, `Snowshoe Hare`, `White-tailed Deer`, `Gray Wolf`) to western longitudes (`-52° W` to `-168° W`), default play spawns **`0` Moose, `0` Black Bears, `0` Beavers, `0` Bald Eagles, `0` Lynx, and `0` Caribou** — leaving only the Holarctic `Red Fox` (`lon_max = +180°`).
2. **`17 / 19` mammal GLB models are rigged `90°` sideways (twisting legs into crooked zigzags), and `Bald Eagle` stands `2.224 m` tall on the dirt**: `fitAnimalModel` (`animalModels.ts:45`) unconditionally rotates every GLB by `-90°` around `Y` assuming the nose faces `-X`. In reality, **`17 / 19` mammal GLBs (`89.5%`, including `White-tailed Deer`, `Moose`, `Elk`, `Caribou`, `Black Bear`, `Grizzly Bear`, `Red Fox`, `Cougar`, `Wolverine`, `Beaver`, `Snowshoe Hare`) face along `Z`**, so `-90°` turns them sideways (`White-tailed Deer` `0.536 m` long × `1.569 m` wide!) and `meshySkin.ts` rigs their front/hind leg bones onto their **left and right flanks**, shearing deer and moose legs into grotesque sideways pretzels (`frame-2.png`, `frame-3.png`). Meanwhile, `fitAnimalModel` scales birds by `box.max.z - box.min.z` (the thin ribcage after `-90°` rotation), blowing **`Bald Eagle` up to `2.224 m` tall** (`3.0x` real perched height, towering over the `1.45 m` Black Bear in `frame-0.png` & `frame-2.png`!) and **`Common Raven` to `1.569 m` tall**, while `WITHERS_SHARE = 0.62` inflates crouching `Beaver` to `0.484 m` tall (`1.73x`).
3. **Wildlife stands in a `1.65 m` police lineup treadmilling at `0.000 m/s` in `0.65 Hz` lockstep, with a grounded walking Eagle, and teleports `10–13 m` whenever the player turns `> 20°`**: `landingCast.sync` lines up predators and prey (`Black Bear` `1.65 m` beside `White-tailed Deer`, `Snowshoe Hare`, `Beaver`, and a ground-walking `0.00 m` AGL `Bald Eagle`) `7.2 m` ahead of the wolf, marching their legs at `speed = 1.20 m/s` (`phase = (time * 0.65) % 1`) while holding `(x, z)` completely frozen (`0.000 m/s` world speed = `100%` foot-slip), and `castShouldReplace` teleports the entire lineup `10–13 m` across the forest whenever player yaw turns past `0.35 rad` (`20.05°`, `frame-1.png`).
4. **Mid-distance (`16–140 m`) animals render as untextured solid-colour `MeshBasicMaterial` rectangles that glow unlit at midnight and leak `100%` opacity onto near animals**: `animalPicture.ts:151–153` builds mid-tier impostors out of `PlaneGeometry(1, 1)` + unlit `MeshBasicMaterial` (no silhouette cutout, skipped by `coatNight`), turning animals into glowing rectangular colour swatches at `00:00` midnight (`frame-3.png`), while `fade.opacity = Math.max(fade.opacity, share.low)` shares **one** material opacity per species so a `48 m` deer slaps a `100%`-opaque `1.57 m × 1.61 m` tan rectangle right over a `18 m` near deer (`frame-3.png` left!). Near `SkinnedMesh` clones force `material.transparent = true` permanently even at `opacity = 1.0`, `proceduralAnimal.ts` fallback (`Canada Lynx`, `Arctic Fox`) uses shadowless `20`-tri `icosphere(0)` blobs, `LANDING_YEAR = 2026` freezes the 10-year Krebs hare–lynx cycle to `1.00x`, and `0 / 3` world systems (`#senses`, `Generation Inspector`, `SnapshotHub`) see `landingCast` fauna.
