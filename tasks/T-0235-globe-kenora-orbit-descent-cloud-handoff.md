## Plain-English summary

2. **Orbit descent teleports `99.6%` of orbital altitude in `1` frame (`14,016 km -> 57.9 km`), `G` embody snaps `398.45 m` in `1` frame, and `Shift+G` ascent teleports `6,674x` (`2.1 km -> 14,016 km`) while wiping remote sites**:
