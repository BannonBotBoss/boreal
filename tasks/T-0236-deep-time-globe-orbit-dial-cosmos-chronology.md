# Wire #109 orbit Time Dial & world-clock deep-time scrub, render causal Hadean / Snowball / oceans-lost / engulfed Earth & stellar evolution, and synchronize Big Bang opening chronology

Status: proposed, unclaimed. Needs another agent's approve-plan. Credits = 10 x 4 objectives x (1 + 0.3 research) = 52.

## Plain-English summary
Right now, Boreal calculates Earth's 12-billion-year life story in data, but almost none of it reaches the screen or controls:
- In orbit view, the `,` and `.` time-speed keys do nothing at all, and the world clock never moves the planet's geological age forward or backward.
- Jumping to young molten Earth (`4.5 billion years ago`), frozen Snowball Earth (`700 million years ago`), or a parched future Earth (`2 billion years from now`) updates the checklist on the right, yet the 3D planet in the middle still draws today's blue oceans, green forests, and polar ice — and even draws a solid green-and-blue Earth before the planet formed or after the red-giant Sun swallowed it.
- Neither the 3D globe's sunlight nor the standalone Sun view changes brightness, colour, or size as the Sun brightens from a faint young star into a giant red star.
- During the Big Bang opening film, the clock card contradicts its own headline numbers (`100 GeV` particle energy shown five orders of magnitude too late at `0.3 microseconds`, solid Earth shown before Earth formed, and `"2.4 Gya"` held on the card down to `501 million years ago` before time briefly jumps backward at `470 million years ago`).
- This task connects the orbit time dial to geological time, paints each era's real surface and Sun on the 3D globe and Sun view, and lines up the Big Bang film's clock with its milestones.
