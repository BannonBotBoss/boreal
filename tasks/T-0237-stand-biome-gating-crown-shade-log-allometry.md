# Stand/flora biome & hydrology gating, home coverSample Kazakhstan steppe bug, broadleaf crown/shade & snag dendrometry, and 1.2 m chainsaw-log allometry/footprints

Status: proposed, unclaimed. Needs another agent's approve-plan. Credits = 10 x 4 objectives x (1 + 0.3 research) = 52.

## Plain-English summary
Four linked systems that place trees, ground plants, tree crowns, and fallen logs break real-world nature in clear ways:
1. **Trees and plants ignore deserts, glaciers, lakes, and Kenora's real location**: A second tree-spawning pass (`applyPopulation`) and the grass/moss layers ignore land-cover classes and staged lakes, planting hundreds of boreal spruce trees, logs, blueberries, moss, and green grass across barren subtropical deserts, high mountain rock/ice, and open lakes and rivers — while default home play samples `+65.5° E` in Kazakhstan and paints Kenora's forest as dry yellow steppe.
2. **Planet land-cover math never creates subtropical deserts and drowns flat inland plains**: Without satellite tiles cached, the procedural globe sets full forest greenness across the entire Sahara/Arabian latitude belt (`15°–33°`) and sets every flat inland landing's ground level `20 cm` below water.
3. **Paper birch crowns and tree shade are shrunk in half, and dead leafless snags drop green leaves**: Distant paper birches draw crowns less than half their real width, understory shade divides crown width by two a second time, and dead leafless tree trunks still count as full leafy crowns that shade the floor and drop fresh green litter every year.
4. **Fallen logs are meter-thick chainsaw-cut cylinders that grass grows straight through**: Every fallen log is `64–120 cm` thick (`3.6×` wider than the `22 cm` trees around it) with a bright flat chainsaw-cut end, never slumps into a mossy mound as it rots over a century, and uses an unrotated east-west rectangle so grass grows right through turned logs while leaving an empty bare scar beside them.
