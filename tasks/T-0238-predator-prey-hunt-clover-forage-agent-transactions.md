## Plain-English summary

1. **Standstill `24.5 g` pounces, mid-air curving leaps, skipped pin phase, `+0.9 rad` stuck running forelegs, `0.00 s` instant swallows, and lost reload bonds**: Pressing Space from a dead stop launches the wolf horizontally at `8.0 m/s` in a single `1/30 s` tick (`240 m/s² = 24.5 g`, `8×` the `30 m/s²` biomechanical limit) and curves its flight path in mid-air at `13 m/s²` (`steerLeap`) with zero paws on the ground. Catching a hare runs `pin`, `bite`, and `hold` synchronously in the same tick (`0.00 s` pin phase), locks both wolf forelegs forward at `+0.90 rad` even while running at `6–10.5 m/s`, swallows a whole `1.5 kg` hare instantaneously in `0.00 s` (`1` chew via `mealChews(bite, bite)`, zero sharing with the 5 following pups), and forgets to save released-hare friendship bonds (`releasePrey` omits `commitContext()`, `hare.ts` never loads saved `bonds`, and `SimWorld.time = 0` on reload inflates `friendStrength` above `1.0`).
