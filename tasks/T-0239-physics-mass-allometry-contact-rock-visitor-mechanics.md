## Plain-English summary
Four connected physics, procedural-mass, contact, and visitor-body systems break physical reality and natural proportions across the forest:
1. **Animals weigh 3–9× too much, blueberry bushes weigh 130 kg, and rotted logs never lose weight**: The procedural mass calculator fills the empty air under an arched animal's belly and antlers with solid muscle and bone (`515 kg` base white-tailed deer, up to **`1,303 kg` and `4.4 m` tall** in the wild; `72 kg` wolf; `4.1 kg` hare), uses an old 7-boulder wood formula that makes every small blueberry bush or wildflower clump weigh **`130.38 kg`**, and keeps 100-year-old rotted logs at fresh-timber density (`512 kg/m³`).
2. **Animals accelerate at 3–8 g on dirt, and ramming a deer breaks conservation of momentum**: Ground acceleration is set to **`30 m/s²` (`3.1 g`)** for the wolf and **`78.2 m/s²` (`8.0 g`)** for the embodied snowshoe hare (`6×` a cheetah's peak!), while player–deer collisions (`const [, vd2] = collide(...)`) throw away the player's own recoil velocity so even a `4 kg` hare or `72 kg` wolf blasts a `515–1,303 kg` deer away while losing **`0.00 m/s`** speed, injecting hundreds of `N·s` of free momentum and firing zero `CONTACTS` events.
3. **Snout rock-pushing slides 13 kg cobbles along a 60° hex grid straight underwater with zero friction**: Pushing a stone (`move`) ignores stone mass, ground friction, soil burial, tree trunks, steep slopes, and waterlines — nudging up to **`13.38 kg` (`29.5 lb`)** cobbles `22 cm` on a fixed `0.55 s` timer along `60°` hex snaps right into open lakes or leaving them perched on cliffs steeper than the angle of repose.
4. **Human Visitors are 2.5 m, 525 kg giant-headed clay statues that glide without leg joints and sink 0.5 m underground to sit**: Spawned `person` entities stand **`2.28–3.19 m` (`7.5–10.5 ft`) tall** and weigh **`423–1,160 kg`** with **`50–70 cm` (`83+ kg`) sphere heads**, glide at `1.4–2.5 m/s` on welded cylinder legs with `0°` stride, plunge their rigid upright legs **`34–63 cm` vertically underground** when sitting (`posture === 'rest'`), spawn un-gated on glaciers and deserts, and walk into staged upland lakes above `0.1 m`.
