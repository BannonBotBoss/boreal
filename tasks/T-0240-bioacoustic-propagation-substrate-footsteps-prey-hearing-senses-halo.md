# 45 m bioacoustic cull & unattenuated spatial cues, substrate-muffled paw footsteps, prey vocal/transient hearing, and HRTF/senses-halo orientation

Status: proposed, unclaimed. Needs another agent's approve-plan. Credits = 10 x 4 objectives x (1 + 0.3 research) = 52.

## Plain-English summary
Four connected bioacoustic, footstep-substrate, prey-hearing, and spatial-sensory systems break how sound travels and how animals hear and see across the boreal forest:
1. **Every sound in the world hits a hard 40–45 m wall, cues never fade with distance, mute makes the wolf deaf, and crying pups are invisible to the hearing HUD**: Common Loon wails (`108 dB` — audible miles across a lake), owl hoots, woodpecker drums, and `200 m` deer alarm snorts are all chopped off at **`40–45 m`**. Inside `40 m`, a sound at `39.9 m` registers in `Grey wolf senses` as **`"loud"` (`0.90` intensity, identical to `0.5 m`)** before dropping to `0` at `40.1 m`. Muting browser audio (or playing before audio unlock) turns off the animal's biological hearing cues, and crying wolf pups (`playPup`) never send cues to `#senses` or the 3D `sensesHalo` ring at all.
2. **Walking or running on 34 cm of soft snow sounds identical to bare granite**: Even though the ground physics engine already computes foot sinkage (`13.1 cm` in `34.4 cm` snow) and impact absorption (`ground.absorb = 0.41` on snow vs `0.95` on bare Shield rock), `BorealSound.paw()` ignores `groundAbsorb` completely — playing every paw step at 100% un-muffled volume and pitch.
3. **Deer and snowshoe hares are completely deaf to wolf howls, growls, and stone clatters, and get zero warning difference between snow and bare rock**: Both NPC perception engines compute player noise solely from movement speed (`speed`). A stationary wolf (`0 m/s`) howling (`~105 dB`) right behind a downwind deer or hare produces **`0.00` sound perception**, while stalking across soft snow or wet moss produces the exact same footfall noise to prey as trotting across bare rock.
4. **Orbiting the camera flips left/right headphone HRTF audio by 90°, blinds the wolf's forward eyes, and omits visual (`sight`) beacons on the 3D `sensesHalo`**: Turning the camera `90°` leaves the WebAudio `AudioListener` stuck on the wolf's body yaw (`90.07°` binaural azimuth error), turning `180°` evaluates the wolf's biological eyes along the camera's backward angle instead of the wolf's head, and `sensesHalo` only draws `sound` and `scent` while ignoring `sensesHUD.sights()`.
