# Embodied HUD health bars & colliding chrome, steer/stick speed loss & off-animal camera pan, missing gamepad/axis bindings, and unclamped/silent haptics

Status: proposed, unclaimed. Needs another agent's approve-plan. Credits = 10 x 4 objectives x (1 + 0.3 research) = 52.

## Plain-English summary
Four connected control, camera-orbit, gamepad-rebinding, haptic, and HUD-chrome defects break how the player steers, looks around, uses a controller, and sees the forest:
1. **Embodied play still shows five raw numeric health/reserve gauges and stacks overlapping buttons over the title, footer, and menus**: Rule 18 (`#102` & `#118`) explicitly states that embodied play has **no health bar** and raw numbers belong only in debug mode. Yet `#status` displays `Stamina 100/100 · Energy 75/100 · Water 100% · Rest 100%` every frame of normal play, `#target` prints raw NPC `Energy`, `<button id="data-panel-btn">Data (U)</button>` (`z-index: 70`) crashes directly on top of `"PROCEDURAL WORLD / 01"` in `<header>` and stays visible over `Photo Mode`, `Globe`, `Controls`, and `Gamepad settings`, `#camera-panel` (`bottom: 8px; right: 8px`) covers the `Library / controls` footer button, pressing `C` to crouch into **Stealth** hijacks `#camera-panel` and pops open the camera exposure drawer, and opening `Controls` leaves the game footer visible underneath.
2. **Turning while moving cuts forward speed by ~30%, the controller stick has a 20.7° dead steering cross, and "orbiting" the camera pans away from the wolf until it disappears off-screen**: `main.ts:612` normalizes yaw steering (`dx`) together with forward drive (`dz`) as if the wolf were 2D planar-strafing, dropping forward speed by **`29.3%`** (`1.00 -> 0.7071`, `2.09 -> 1.40 m/s` live) whenever turning with `W+A`/`W+D`. On gamepad, `bindings.ts:166–168` applies `DEADZONE = 0.18` independently to `x` and `y` instead of radially, creating a **`20.74°` dead steering cross** (`0.0000` steering at `10°` off-forward) and `-9.1%` (`-35.7%` vs full drive) diagonal loss. Worse, `positionCamera` (`render/math.ts:38–43`) keeps `camera.position` fixed behind the tail and adds `lookYaw` only to `camera.lookAt`, so dragging `60°` horizontally to "orbit" pans the wolf completely outside the screen (`ndcX = -2.1132`, visible in only `7 / 37` = `18.9%` of `0..180°` orbit angles), limits Right-Stick look to a sluggish `1.0 rad/s` (`57.3°/s`), and snaps mouse-orbited views back to `0°` within `0.65 s` of letting go of the mouse.
3. **Controller players cannot eat or release held prey, 12 `Axis` controls are missing from `Gamepad settings`, and 3 UI modules ignore saved rebindings**: `eatPrey`, `releasePrey`, `howl`, `growl`, `pause`, `photo`, and `planet` all have `pad: []` (`24 / 31` embodied/global actions bound), so a gamepad player holding a pinned hare sees `"— eat held · — release"` and has zero controller buttons to eat or release it. Meanwhile `CONTEXTS` (`bindings.ts:25`) omits `'axis'`, hiding all **`12` `axis*` actions** (`72 / 84` reachable) from `Gamepad settings`, `Controls` dumps `87` ungrouped rows without section headers, and `timeHud.ts`, `menu.ts`, and `dataPanel.ts` hardcode raw keys/buttons instead of reading `BINDINGS_KEY`.
4. **Gamepad rumble exceeds the W3C `[0, 1]` spec limit, WebXR haptics are disconnected, snow footsteps rumble identically to bare rock, and the `#109` `±1×` time-dial detent has zero tactile click**: `rumble()` (`haptics.ts:94`) passes unclamped `strongMagnitude = 1.11` (`> 1.0`) to `playEffect('dual-rumble')`, `setXRSession` has `0` callers in `src/vr/session.ts`, `stride` rumble ignores `ground.absorb` (`1.00` ratio on `34 cm` snow vs bare granite instead of `0.4285`), and crossing the `#109` `±1×` real-time lip in `nudgeDial()` emits `0` tactile pulses despite `#109` having no visual time bar.
