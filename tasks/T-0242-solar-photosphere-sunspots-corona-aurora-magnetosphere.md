# Clipped solar photosphere & missing bloom/OutputPass, blank post-2026-09 Cycle-25 sunspots & zero B0 tilt, grey 550 nm spots & locked EUV passbands, and 100 km clipped aurora & uncompressed dipole magnetosphere

Status: proposed, unclaimed. Needs another agent's approve-plan. Credits = 10 x 4 objectives x (1 + 0.3 research) = 52.

## Plain-English summary
Four connected solar-physics, spectral-radiometry, coronal-passband, and solar-wind magnetosphere/aurora defects prevent Boreal's Sun and space-weather pipeline from resembling nature:
1. **`?view=sun` is unwired in `entry.ts`, and `sunView.ts` accidentally commented out `UnrealBloomPass` and `OutputPass`, blowing `65.8%` of the solar disc to flat `[255, 255, 255]` white**: Opening `/?view=sun` boots the wolf forest (`main.ts`) instead of `startSunView()` because `src/world/globe/entry.ts` omits `view === 'sun'`. Worse, on `src/world/sun/sunView.ts:62`, `; composer.addPass(bloom); composer.addPass(new OutputPass());` sit **inside a `//` end-of-line comment**! Because `EffectComposer` runs only `RenderPass`, neither Spencer et al. (1995) off-limb veiling glare (`0.0000` at `rho = 1.03`) nor `ACESFilmicToneMapping` + sRGB gamma encoding executes: raw linear `col * uExpo` (`1.50` at disc centre) clips hard at `1.0` across **`65.84%`** of the solar disc (`rho = 0.00..0.70`), erasing Neckel & Labs (1994) centre-to-limb darkening and washing out `5450 K` sunspot penumbrae.
2. **The Sun has `0` sunspots on any date past `2026-09-30` (including today `2026-11-15`), `0°` Carrington `B_0` axial tilt, frozen non-scrubbable groups, and a private xorshift PRNG**: `public/data/silso/SN_d_tot_V2.0.csv` ends on `2026-09-30`, and `groupsOn()` returns `[]` whenever `silso.get(day)` is `undefined` — turning Solar Cycle 25 near maximum (`~5.3–9.0` expected bipolar groups in November 2026 per Hathaway 2015) into a completely blank disc (`SILSO R=- groups=0`). Meanwhile `sunView.ts:71` hardcodes Carrington heliographic disc-centre latitude `B_0 = 0°` (ignoring the `7.25°` solar-equator inclination to the ecliptic), `sunspots.ts:35` defines a private `function rng(seed)` violating `PROJECT-RULES.md` Rule 2 (`src/proc/rng.ts`), `uAim` pan is unwired, and `spots`/`loops` never update with the world clock.
3. **Sunspots render with neutral-grey `550 nm` scalar contrast instead of warm `3900 K` Planck colours, `7 / 8` solar passbands are locked behind `"quiz": "todo"`, and `EUV 171 Å` loops collapse onto 2D chords on an un-gamma-encoded `RGB(15, 12, 4)` disc**: `sunView.ts:58` passes scalar `contrast(3900, 550)` to all RGB channels (`1.00` shader R/B ratio vs `1.8247` Planck `610 nm / 465 nm` contrast ratio), and `sunView.ts:66` divides all channels by `limbMean(550)` (`2.313e9 cd/m²` disc-centre luminance vs `2.00e9 cd/m²` observed). In normal play (without dev `?unlockAll=1`), `cameraPanel.ts:16–17` resets `?band=euv171` to `'visible'` and locks `7 / 8` SDO/AIA & ground passbands plus manual exposure behind unimplemented `"quiz": "todo"` gates (`0` `earn()` call sites in `src/`). Even with `?unlockAll=1`, missing `OutputPass` leaves the `171 Å` Fe IX quiet corona `4.6×` too dark (`RGB(15, 12, 4)` = `0.0588` raw linear vs `0.269` sRGB), and `sunView.ts:78–82` collapses all `3` magnetic loops per group onto one 2D chord `A -> B` (`0` azimuthal dipole fan).
4. **Globe off-limb `110–400 km` aurora is razor-clipped at `Ra = Rp + 100 km`, on-disk aurora is dimmed `6.25×` (`0.16`), `omniNow` & midnight oval shift are static, and `L = 8 R_E` dayside dipole lines ignore the `5.06 R_E` Gannon magnetopause**: `atmosphere.ts:343` builds the sky dome with `SphereGeometry(Ra * 1000)` (`Ra = Rp + 100 km`) even though `atmEmission()` (`line 159`) marches `Rp..Rp + 400 km` for `110 km` green (`557.7 nm`) and `250 km` red (`630.0 nm`) aurora — slicing off every off-limb auroral curtain above `100 km`! On the planet disk (`line 403`), `atmEmission(rd, d) * ATM_AERIAL` attenuates aurora by `0.16×` and gates it on Rayleigh scatter (`uSteps.z`). Finally, `globeView.ts:242–250` freezes `omniNow` at one static hour (`omniPeak`), omits Feldstein & Starkov (1967) `~4.5°` midnight equatorward oval shift, and draws symmetric vacuum dipole lines out to `L = 8.0 R_E` on the dayside even when `shueR0()` compresses the subsolar magnetopause to `5.059 R_E`.
