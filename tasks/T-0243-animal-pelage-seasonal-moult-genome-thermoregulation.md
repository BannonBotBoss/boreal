## Plain-English summary
Four coupled animal-pelage, coat-shading, appearance-genome, and endotherm-thermoregulation defects prevent Boreal's mammals and birds from resembling nature across seasons, day/night lighting, individual genetics, and winter/water exposure:
1. **Snowshoe Hares and Ermines render summer-brown in January snow, and `15 / 21` stored Meshy species still apply `14x` unmapped emissive blowout**: `HARE_SKIN` (`src/animals/hare.ts:128–136`, `src/render/rigSkin.ts:71–86`) implements photoperiod seasonal moult (`seasonalWhiteness = 1.000` on Jan 15 per Mills et al. 2013 *PNAS* 110:7360), yet `public/models/animals/manifest.json` assigns static summer-brown GLBs to `"Snowshoe Hare"` and `"Ermine"`, and `src/render/animalPicture.ts:202–233` hides the procedural rig (`proc = 0`) inside `32 m` — drawing brown summer hares and ermines on `34.4 cm` January snow (`frame-0.png`). Worse, `src/render/meshySkin.ts:469–472` only exempts `Red Fox`, `/wolf/i`, and `cervidName` from `applyStylizedFill(material)` (`src/render/stylizedPalette.ts:67–72`: `m.toneMapped = false`, `m.emissive.setRGB(1, 0.94, 0.82)`, `m.emissiveIntensity = 14`), leaving **`15 / 21` manifest species** (`Snowshoe Hare`, `Ermine`, `Coyote`, `Gray Fox`, `Cougar`, `Striped Skunk`, `Northern Raccoon`, `Wolverine`, `River Otter`, `American Mink`, `Black Bear`, `Grizzly Bear`, `Beaver`, `Bald Eagle`, `Common Raven`) blown out by `14x` self-luminous warm emissive lift!
2. **Red Fox and Cervid coats ignore `outgoingLight` (glowing full-bright at `00:00` midnight), `MAMA` erases Gray Wolf colour morphs, and Cervids lack winter coat moult**: `FOX` (`src/render/coatFinish.ts:62–66, 77`: `toneMapped = false`, `gl_FragColor.rgb = fox`) and `dressCervidCoat` (`src/render/coatFinish.ts:100–108`: `gl_FragColor.rgb = coat`) overwrite `gl_FragColor.rgb` **without multiplying by `outgoingLight`**, causing Red Fox (`frame-2.png`) and Moose/Elk/Deer/Caribou to glow at `100%` daytime brightness in pitch-dark midnight (`hour = 0.05`) and cast-shadowed forest! Meanwhile `MAMA` (`src/render/coatFinish.ts:20–28`) overwrites every Meshy wolf's `gl_FragColor.rgb` with one hardcoded charcoal/cream palette (`[0.16..0.46]`), wiping out `wolfCoat(seed)`'s black melanistic ($K^B$), grizzled-grey, and golden-tan morphs (`src/core/body.ts:186–195`, which also uses a legacy `Math.sin(seed * 12.9898)` hash instead of `src/proc/rng.ts`), and `dressCervidCoat` bakes a static summer rufous-tan RGB literal with `0%` winter grey-brown hollow-hair shift (Jacobsen 1980).
3. **The `Appearance` genome editor breaks on `player:wolf`, fails to scale or tint visible Meshy skins or hares, clobbers `onBeforeCompile`, and leaves giant cyan drag spheres stuck on the wolf after resume**: Once `gray-wolf.glb` loads, `setBodyShare(wolf.root, 0)` hides the procedural wolf's meshes, so `baseProfile(root)` (`src/render/appearance.ts:21`: `if (!o.visible) return;`) returns `0` points (`min = +Infinity, size = -Infinity`) on hidden rigs (`frame-1.png` captures only fragmentary dots). `setAppearance` (`src/render/buildScene.ts:656–660`) mutates only hidden `wolf.root` and `deer` rigs — never scaling or tinting the visible `createWolfLod` / `animalPicture` Meshy skins or `hares` (`0.00` visual response to `size = 1.3`, `hue`, or painted `coat` marks), while `inject()` (`appearance.ts:42`) overwrites `m.onBeforeCompile` without calling `prev` (wiping CSM shadow & coat hooks). Worse, `appearancePanel.ts:56` defines `alive: () => document.contains(root)`, while `menu.close()` (`src/ui/menu.ts:106`) only sets `root.hidden = true` without removing panel children — leaving unoccluded `0x4ad0ff` cyan `shapeTools` spheres (`depthTest: false`, `renderOrder = 1000`) permanently glued to the wolf and intercepting clicks in live play (`frame-2.png`, `frame-3.png`)!
4. **Animal coats have `0%` wet darkening/clumping, `0%` snow dusting, `0` `windAt` fur motion, and `0.000` cold or wet-coat thermoregulatory cost even at `-30 °C` or in icy water**: Unlike terrain (`vWet`, `vSnow`), animal pelage (`coatFinish.ts`, `furCards.ts`, `rigSkin.ts`) stays `100%` dry when swimming/wading or in rain (`0%` Lekner & Dorf 1988 ~38% wet albedo darkening or guard-hair clumping), accumulates `0%` dorsal snow during snowfall (`frame-3.png`), and never samples `windAt(x, y, z)` in `furCards.ts` despite `src/core/weather.ts:14` explicitly specifying fur wind coupling.
