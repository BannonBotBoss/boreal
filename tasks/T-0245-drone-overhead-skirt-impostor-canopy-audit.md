## Plain-English summary
Four coupled radiometric, spatial-ecology, biome-gating, and coordinate-alignment defects prevent Boreal's 135–2,200 m drone, glide, horizon-skirt, mid-distance impostor, and nadir overhead views (`?transition=inpage` and `globeView`) from resembling the real world:
1. **At `00:00` astronomical midnight (`-26.2°` sun), `180–2,200 m` globe land glows uniform daylight olive-brown (`tile.ts:569–580`), `overheadCrowns` pastes an unlit green moss lawn directly over open lakes and stamps `336` crisp directional sun shadows in the dark, and `horizonMesh` + `inpage.ts` paint the midnight drone horizon blinding daylight white-blue**: Whenever camera altitude is in `(180, 2200)` m, `src/render/trees/overheadCrowns.ts:289` sets `AERIAL.uCrownFloor.value = 1` **before** checking water or biome (`line 301`), triggering `src/world/globe/tile.ts:569–580` (`#include <dithering_fragment>`, **after lighting and ACES tone-mapping**) to overwrite `gl_FragColor.rgb = floorD` (`~0.24–0.40` sRGB) across every land pixel on Earth — erasing solar illumination, the day/night terminator, and winter snowpack so `97.12%` of the `450 m` midnight frame exceeds `0.20` sRGB luma (`mean luma = 0.2640` in `frame-0.png` vs `<= 0.0040` nocturnal target). Simultaneously, `overheadCrowns.ts:222–235, 311–330` draws a `795.3 m × 447.4 m` `FLOOR_FRAG` plane (`toneMapped: false`, `depthTest: false`) across open lakes (`0%` lake-water exclusion), scales spruce cones to squat `3.36–6.05 m` domes (`mean 4.70 m` vs `12–20 m` mature *Picea mariana*/*glauca*), and stamps `336` black `0.62 * d` sun shadows (`overhead-shadows`) under a `-26.14°` midnight sun! In the `band` drone view (`frame-2.png`), `src/render/horizonMesh.ts:21, 93–100` mixes constant `uSkirtHazeColor = RGB(0.78, 0.86, 0.98)` (`setHaze` has `0` callers across the codebase) **after tone-mapping** across distant land and `85%` grazing water (`peak horizon row sRGB luma = 0.7873` at `00:03` midnight!), `src/world/globe/inpage.ts:476` forces `scene.fog.color.setRGB(0.45, 0.62, 0.90)` whenever `8 < h < 2500`, and `horizonCover.ts:50` + `overheadCrowns.ts:253–254` apply unconditional green `emissive` (`STYLIZED.foliageLift = [0.028, 0.042, 0.018]`) so foliage self-luminesces in pitch darkness.
2. **Mid-distance `horizonCover` plants `4,096` stored `.glb` tree cards on an unjittered `6 m × 6 m` military orchard lattice (`Clark–Evans R = 2.0000`), marches `52.59%` of them straight across submerged water, truncates abruptly in a razor-sharp semicircular cliff at `219.7 m` (`5.81%` of the `36–900 m` annulus), and crushes/double-lights baked noon sprites**: `src/world/globe/coverKind.ts:96–111` generates `70,564` unjittered `(k * 6, m * 6)` lattice points ($R = 2.0000$ vs natural boreal fire-origin spruce stands $R \approx 0.78\text{–}1.02$) and `slice(0, 4096)` cuts off at $r = 219.72\text{ m}$ (`frame-1.png`), leaving `220–900 m` completely bare before `horizonMesh.ts:68` far crowns reach full weight (`900 m`). `src/render/horizonCover.ts:114` places stems at raw `heightAt(s.x, s.z)` with **zero water check** (`2,154 / 4,096 = 52.59%` planted below `LEVEL.waterSurface`), uses unseeded `Math.sin` (`PROJECT-RULES.md` Rule 2 violation), and aims flat cards horizontally (`lookAt(cx, y + 4, cz)`). Worse, `src/render/trees/cc0Impostors.ts:12–18, 64–107, 128–135` loads `5` external `/models/trees/*.glb` assets (`PROJECT-RULES.md` Rule 1 violation, despite procedural `trees.ts` + `impostor.ts` already existing), bakes hardcoded noon `DirectionalLight` + `HemisphereLight` into an untonemapped linear `WebGLRenderTarget` and tags the pixels `THREE.SRGBColorSpace` (`0.16^{2.2} = 0.0177` gamma crush), then feeds them into `MeshStandardMaterial` with unrotated bake-camera view-space `MeshNormalMaterial` normals (`horizonCover.ts:68–69`) — blowing out sunward card faces cyan-white (`frame-1.png`).
3. **`horizonMesh` binds `uForest` (`0` on prairie/desert/tundra/ice/rock) without ever reading it in GLSL — stamping boreal conifer `uCanopy` across every desert and prairie — while `horizonCover` and `overheadCrowns` spawn temperate `oak.glb`/`broadleaf.glb` trees and green moss floors on arid deserts**: `src/render/horizonMesh.ts:40, 48, 150` computes `made.forest.value = 0` ("A prairie photograph does not grow one") and declares `uniform float uForest`, **but `uForest` is never read anywhere in the fragment shader** (`0` shader reads)! Lines `68–92` unconditionally stamp `22.2 m`-repeating `uCanopy` conifer blobs (`45%` mix) and `12 m` `0.62..1.28` crown-gap contrast across `400–14,000 m` over arid deserts, prairies, tundra, alpine rock, and glacier ice (`frame-3.png`, alongside concentric ring-boundary geomorph seams). Meanwhile `horizonCover.ts:30–33, 109` skips only `'prairie'`, so `'desert'` (`step = 70 m`) spawns **`516` mesic `broadleaf.glb` (`180`) and `oak.glb` (`336`) trees** across desert regolith (`frame-3.png`), and `coverTreeShare('desert') = (6/70)^2 = 0.007347 > 0` keeps `overheadCrowns` `FLOOR_FRAG` (`overheadBorealFloorVisibleOnDesert = true`) active over deserts!
4. **`aerialExemplar.ts` decodes sRGB NAIP PNGs as `THREE.NoColorSpace` (`2.77×` gamma distortion) and blends North American boreal spruce/fen crops globally across all non-ice Earth biomes, while `planet.ts` hashes `homeLon` into Kazakhstan (`49.78° N, 65.53° E`) so `openTiles.ts` drapes Eurasian steppe EOX imagery over the Kenora boreal forest**: `src/world/globe/aerialExemplar.ts:29, 55` leaves `tex.colorSpace = THREE.NoColorSpace` on 8-bit sRGB NAIP PNGs (`110/255 = 0.4314` vs IEC 61966-2-1 linear `0.1559`), compensated by ad-hoc `uExGain = 2.5`, and `src/world/globe/tile.ts:401–455` blends those boreal conifer/fen/burn crops across `100%` of non-ice land biomes globally (`1 - clamp(vCover.w, 0, 1)`). Finally, `src/world/globe/planet.ts:31, 146` fixes Kenora's latitude (`49.77° N`) while hashing longitude from `WORLD_SEED` to `65.53° E` (Kazakhstan semi-arid steppe instead of Kenora `-94.49° W`), so `openTiles.ts` fetches real-Earth Kazakh steppe EOX imagery and `coverSampleAt` misclassifies the wolf's home boreal forest as `'prairie'` (`seededHomeClassifiedBiome = "prairie"`, wiping out `horizonCover` trees at home), while `vegetationWeight` (`planet.ts:244–270`) lacks any subtropical Hadley aridity term off-EOX.
