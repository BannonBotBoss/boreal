# Drained home base-water lake craters & 100m horizon water-sheet cliff, backface-culled underwater sky & 0.55 midnight/non-advecting wakes, D8 step-boulder stranding & bank over-meshing, and forested remote Great Lakes

Status: proposed, unclaimed. Needs another agent's approve-plan. Credits = 10 x 4 objectives x (1 + 0.3 research) = 52.

## Plain-English summary
Four coupled hydrological, optical, fluid-advective, and planetary land-cover defects prevent Boreal's lakes, rivers, oceans, wakes, underwater camera views, and remote globe-landing waterbodies from resembling the real world:
1. **At home (`!SITE.remote`), `99.40%` (`12,276 / 12,350`) of Kenora's base-water lakes (`height < -0.25 m`, `27%` of the map) have ZERO water surface mesh — turning `19.22 m`-deep lakes beside spawn into dry khaki-olive craters — and even where water renders, `mountChunkWater` cuts off abruptly at `INTEREST.mid = 100 m` (`1.57%` of horizon radius, `0.0245%` of horizon area) without Earth `curvatureDrop` or `addAerialPerspective`**: On the staged home map (`seed = 1400`), `lakeGrid` (`src/core/stages/elevation.ts:154`) and `riverPondGrid` skip every basin connected to base-water outlets (`height <= LEVEL.waterSurface = -0.25 m`), so `terrainCore` builds `waterGeo = null` and relies on `open-water-sheet`. Yet `src/render/buildScene.ts:180` gates `openSheet` on `drawOpenWater(x, z)` (`src/world/globe/coverSample.ts:81`), which queries coarse planetary / Kazakh-steppe `coverSampleAt` (`s.water || s.coastM <= 40`) and returns **`false` across `99.40%` (`12,276 / 12,350`) of home open-water points** (`0 / 49` base-water chunks around `(-40, -340)` in `frame-0.png`, leaving a `19.22 m`-deep `soil: 'water'` abyss completely dry while `surfaceAt` / `isDryLand` suppress all terrestrial flora). Furthermore, both `staged-water` and `open-water-sheet` exist only on chunks within `INTEREST.mid = 100 m` (`buildScene.ts:180, 236–239`), whereas `continuousTerrain` draws the bare khaki-olive bed (`0x929771`) out to `horizonMetres(eyeM)` (`6,385.49 m` at `3.2 m` eye; `8,666.37 m` from the `102.6 ha` lakeshore in `frame-1.png`, where `610 m` of the `710 m` lake fetch — and `99.975%` of ocean landing horizon discs — appear as drained dry dirt), while `chunkWater.ts:34` omits `curvatureDrop(r)` (`3.20 m` at `6.39 km`) and `water.ts` omits `addAerialPerspective(shader)`.
2. **Submerging into any perched lake or deep river backface-culls 100% of the overhead water surface (`THREE.FrontSide`, `0°` Snell's window, `Kd = 0 m^-1`) while the wolf walks on the lake floor under a dry blue sky, `water.ts:50` injects unconditional `0.55` midnight wake bioluminescence, and `waterWake.ts` applies `0 m/s` river advection**: Because `positionCamera` (`src/render/math.ts:32, 38`) sets `ground = Math.max(heightAt(x, z), -0.2)` with zero water-surface clamp or buoyancy and `waterMaterial()` (`src/render/water.ts:25`) defaults to `THREE.FrontSide` with zero underwater Beer–Lambert medium extinction (`Kd = 0` vs `0.6–2.5 m^-1` in Experimental Lakes Area / Kenora humic Shield lakes), walking into the `23.37 m` perched lake (`frame-2.png`, `camY = 22.16 m`, `1.21 m` underwater) culls 100% of overhead water triangles — showing zero `48.61°` Snell critical refraction window, zero total internal reflection, and unattenuated dry subaerial blue sky (`mean sRGB = [0.8559, 0.9014, 0.9256]`) and clouds from underwater! Meanwhile, `water.ts:50` adds `totalEmissiveRadiance += vec3(0.55) * crest` unconditionally regardless of solar/sky irradiance (a noon-sky mirror workaround), causing freshwater wakes at `-26.15°` astronomical midnight (`frame-3.png`) to self-emit `0.55` linear radiance, and `src/core/waterWake.ts:201–238, 258–304` evaluates rings and Kelvin wakes in static ground coordinates `(x - rx, z - rz)` with `0 m/s` downstream advection by `vFlow.xy` even though **`95.11%` (`253 / 266`) of `seed = 1400` curved river reaches exceed capillary-gravity group speed `WAVE.cg = 0.2120 m/s`** (up to `1.194 m/s = 5.63 × cg`), making ripples propagate upstream against supercritical river flow.
3. **`stepsOnSegment` strands `0.95–1.65 m` Shield step boulders on dry unfilleted D8 chords outside curved `RiverPlan` channels, while `terrainCore` marks `inR = rv.d < rv.half + rv.bank` (`2.6906×` channel width)**: `src/core/stages/channelRough.ts:42–50` places steep-reach (`S > 0.02`) step boulders along straight unfilleted D8 grid chords (`segmentGeom`), whereas `riverAt`, `carve`, and `breachUnconfined` follow the filleted/meandered `RiverPlan` (`R_c / W = 2.43`), stranding step boulders outside `rv.d <= rv.half` on dry bend corners while starving curved rapids of step boulders and lee aeration. Simultaneously, `src/render/terrainCore.ts:98` sets `inR = !!rv && rv.d < rv.half + rv.bank` (`374` wet samples vs `139` actual channel samples `rv.d < rv.half`, `2.6906×` over-width), assigning `wet = 1`, `wp.y = rv.surface`, and nonzero `aFlow` across the sloped dry bank zone `d ∈ [half, half + bank]`.
4. **Remote globe landings (`SITE.remote = true`) classify real-Earth inland Great Lakes (`Lake Superior 183 m`, `Lake Winnipeg 217 m`, `Lake Baikal 455 m`) as closed `'boreal'` spruce forest with `water = false`, disable `100%` of rivers off home, and flatten every seeded remote lake to a uniform `0.35 m` puddle**: In `src/world/globe/openTiles.ts:182–185`, `groundForCover(s, demM)` sets `water = false` whenever a real-Earth lake lies above sea level (`demM > 0.5 m`) on a procedural land cell (`s.kind === ''`), then feeds dark blue EOX Sentinel-2 open-water pixels (`G > R`, `RGB ≈ [0.006, 0.014, 0.028]`) into `vegetationFromPhoto` (`openTiles.ts:212–217`), producing **`vegetation = 0.850–0.879` (`>= 0.62` -> `coverKind = 'boreal'`)** and planting closed boreal forest across Lake Superior, Lake Winnipeg, and Lake Baikal! Meanwhile `SITE.remote` forces `lakeAt`, `pondAt`, and `riverAt` to return `undefined` (`elevation.ts:173`, `rivers.ts:121, 176` -> `0` remote rivers) and `src/world/globe/landingSite.ts:66` hardcodes `LEVEL.waterSurface - 0.35` (`35 cm` flat depth) for all seeded remote lakes.
