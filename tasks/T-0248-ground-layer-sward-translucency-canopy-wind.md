# `continuousTerrain` dropped `onBeforeCompile` & `92%` boreal moss/lichen overwrite + missing Layer-1 GI bounce, nocturnal vegetation `STYLIZED` emissive glow, frozen straw steppe sward & `8 m` ring cutoff + desert thatch, and unshadowed leaf translucency & canopy wind shear

## Plain-English summary
Four coupled ground-surface, nocturnal-vegetation, steppe-sward, and canopy-optics/biomechanics bugs keep Boreal's forest floor, night woods, spring grasslands, backlit clearings, and windblown trees from looking and behaving like nature:
1. **The visible forest floor drops its entire moss, lichen, soil-detail, and snow/wetness shader, overwrites `92%` of reindeer lichen under the player's feet, and contributes zero ground bounce light**: `continuousMaterial()` clones `terrainMaterial()` via Three.js `.clone()`, which resets `onBeforeCompile` to an empty no-op — so none of the reindeer-lichen/feathermoss/peat mats (`moss.ts`), procedural micro-normal/roughness detail, wet/snow shading, or cloud shadows ever compile onto the visible ground. Even inside `terrainMaterial()` itself, a second boreal-litter pass overwrites `92%` of the lichen and peat mats inside `10.5 m` around the camera, and because chunk ground plates are hidden (`visible = false`) while `continuousTerrain` is left off Layer 1, the spherical-harmonics bounce-light probe sees empty sky downward instead of forest floor.
2. **Grass blades and black-spruce needles glow radioactive green on a moonless midnight**: Grass (`grassLift`) and `trees=v2` black-spruce needles and bark (`foliageLift`, `barkLift`) add fixed daytime emissive brightness that is never dimmed at night (`coatNight` only traverses animal meshes), making grass carpets and conifer crowns shine like neon green lamps under `0.001 lx` starlight.
3. **Spring feather-grass tussocks are dead September yellow straw, stop abruptly in an `8 m` circle around the camera, stand rigid as iron in gale winds, and paint barren deserts as grass thatch**: In May and June (`steppeCure = 0`), *Stipa* tussocks still draw hardcoded cured September straw over green spring turf, cut off sharply `8 m` from the camera (`~3.8 m` ahead of the wolf's nose) leaving bare smooth hills to the horizon, never sway in wind or bury under snow, and `setGroundFloor('desert')` turns on dense prairie grass thatch across barren deserts.
4. **Backlit grass and leaves glow bright yellow-green inside dark tree-trunk shadows, while fresh breezes shear tall spruce crowns `8.6–13.6 m` sideways without bending normals or dropping crown height**: Subsurface leaf/grass translucency (`addTranslucency`) adds `100%` of direct sunlight into `outgoingLight` without checking shadow maps or cloud shadows (and applies leaf translucency to woody shrub stems while omitting `trees=v2` needles), and tree wind sway shears vertices horizontally by `0.0016 · y²` up to `uWindAmp = 4` (`34%–43%` of tree height at `8 m/s`!) with zero vertical arc-length foreshortening and unrotated normals.
