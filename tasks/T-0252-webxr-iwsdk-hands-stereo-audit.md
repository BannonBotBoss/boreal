# WebXR / IWSDK globe entry & session cleanup, seated journey overlap/scrub/drop & full-fovea blackout vignettes, hand-pose anthropometry & hands-only wolf actions, and stereo `xrOrigin` world-space & Quest `72/90 Hz` governor

Status: proposed, unclaimed. Needs another agent's approve-plan. Credits = 10 x 4 objectives x (1 + 0.3 research) = 52.

## Plain-English summary
Four connected virtual-reality, hand-tracking, stereoscopic-forest, and Quest-headset defects prevent Boreal's seated hands-only Quest 3 journey (Issues `#285` and `#287`, `PROJECT-RULES.md` Rule 10) from working naturally from the opening sphere down into embodied wolf life:
1. **The standalone planet view hides `Enter VR`, desktop clicks spin up a second 3D engine before checking for a headset, and leaving VR leaks the first engine and breaks re-entry**: `?view=globe` never mounts or shows `Enter VR`, clicking `Enter VR` without a headset builds a full second graphics context before discovering no headset is connected, and handing the session to the forest or exiting VR leaves the old `#iwsdk-root` overlay and graphics context behind so `Enter VR` cannot start cleanly a second time.
2. **The seated cosmos-to-planet room skips its opening hand gesture, covers the sphere with raw LaTeX code, snaps time back on release, resets globe rotation, drops on empty-air pinches into `10 s` of pitch blackness, and dims central vision instead of peripheral edges**: The opening room starts cooling immediately instead of waiting for a pinch or open palm, centres the equation card directly in front of the glowing sphere with raw `\frac{...}` code cut off mid-line, snaps scrubbed time back on release, wipes hand rotation of the globe every frame, drops into `10 s` of empty darkness (instead of `#285`'s `14 s` glide over the planet to `400 m`), and covers `100%` of central vision with a flat dark sheet instead of a clear-centre comfort ring.
3. **Fixed centimetre hand thresholds misread a relaxed Quest 3 hand as an open grab and reject large fists, left-hand snap-turn can only turn one way, and hands-only forest play is stuck at a walk with no running, stealth, foraging, or hunting (`#287`)**: Measuring raw wrist-to-fingertip centimetres (`12 cm` open, `9 cm` fist) treats a normal relaxed adult hand (`18–19 cm`) as `'open'` on frame 1 while rejecting a large closed fist (`~10 cm`), 1-frame tracking drops trigger false grabs, left-hand snap-turn checks room-origin `x < 0` instead of left/right of the head, and hands-only play cannot run, sneak, eat berries, lock/pounce prey, or read 2D screen notices inside the headset.
4. **Parenting the headset camera to `xrOrigin` strands terrain, grass, flowers, sky, rain, and bounce light `68 m` away at `(0, 0)`, corrupts the left stereo eye on resize/IBL cube steps, floats the wolf at human chair height (`1.22 m`) behind a grey ball, and runs the quality governor at `60 fps` instead of Quest `72/90 Hz`**: All seven world-space systems read local `camera.position` `(0, 1.22, 0)` instead of world `(65.4, 3.75, -18.6)`, leaving the forest landing bare dirt (`0` grass blades, `1` plant) while `setPixelRatio` / `slicedCube` during presentation blanks the left eye, `xrOrigin` omits the `-0.50 m` wolf eye-height offset (`0.72 m` vs `1.22 m`) and floats a detached grey sphere `xrMuzzle`, `targetGlow` blits a monoscopic outline across both eyes, and `QUEST_FPS = 60` (`16.67 ms`) misses Quest 3's `72/90 Hz` (`13.89 / 11.11 ms`) frame budget.
