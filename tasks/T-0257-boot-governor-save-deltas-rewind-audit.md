# Fix globe resume polar clamp (`+90°N`), cross-seed save/`clearWorldSaves` leaks, `Graphics` split-brain & vsync GPU-bound governor raise, `deltas` grazed-eviction/`prune` crash/wet rebound, and `interest` spike-lock & snapshot rewind wipe

Status: proposed, unclaimed. Needs another agent's approve-plan. Credits = `10 × 4 objectives × (1 + 0.3 research) = 52`.

## Plain-English summary
Four coupled infrastructure, persistence, adaptive-quality, and simulation-lifecycle defects affect booting, saving/resuming, changing worlds or graphics settings, ecological delta persistence, and photo-mode rewind:

1. **Saving from the globe teleports Resume to the North/South Pole (`+90°` / `-90°`), starting a New World leaks old seed/clock/season/species saves, and `releasePrey` / ground-start `pagehide` drop saves**: `captureResume()` (`src/main.ts:495`) passes the planet-frame camera position in metres (`~2.99e7 m`, unnormalized) directly into `toLatLon([cam.x, cam.y, cam.z])`, which clamps `y` to `±1` (`±90.0°` pole, `40.23°` latitude error from Kenora `49.77°N`); `parseContext`/`createContext` (`src/core/events.ts:29,135`) hardcode `WORLD_SEED = 1400` instead of the live `STAGES.seed`, `newWorld` never removes `boreal.universe.seed.v1` (so the old universe seed silently overrides the user's new legacy seed on reload), `clearWorldSaves()` (`src/main.ts:208–212`) leaks `boreal.clock.v1`, `boreal.day.v1`, and `boreal.player.v1` (verified live: a brand-new world inherited day 15 and spawned as a deer!), `releasePrey` (`src/main.ts:408`) never calls `commitContext()`, and `pagehide` (`src/main.ts:317`) skips `saveAll()` whenever `!hasSeenBigBang(context)`.
2. **`Menu -> Graphics` has a split-brain quality selector with unreadable white-on-white `<select>`/`<input>` contrast, GPU-bound 60 fps vsync fools `governFrame` into raising `probeCap`, and startup `?start=globe` blocks for 45+ s on ground terrain while leaking `JOB_FIRST` and running per-frame `JSON.stringify(applied())`**: The top `Quality` dropdown (`src/ui/menu.ts:96–102`) mutates `GOVERNOR.auto`/`tier`/`probeCap` directly without updating `boreal.render.v1` or `GOVERNOR.capTier` (so picking Manual `Ultra` leaves `quality.auto = true`, `quality.tier = "Medium"`, and `applied()` clamped at `Medium`, while `<select>`/`<input>` text renders pale white-on-white and the panel clips off-screen); `governFrame` (`src/core/quality.ts:89–104`) ignores `gpuMs` whenever `frameMs <= budgetMs * 1.1` (`16.67 ms` vsync), reporting `workMs = 3 ms` as `333.3 fps` even when `gpuMs = 15.8 ms` and falsely lifting `probeCap` into overload; `measureHardware` (`src/render/startup.ts:163`) benchmarks with `capTier` still clamping `applied()`; `PROBE.budgetMs` (`src/core/probe.ts:20`) ignores `GOVERNOR.targetFps`; `?start=globe&noprobe=1` waited >48.5 s stuck on `"Building the ground here…"` (`frame-0.png`); `main.ts:707` never clears `JOB_FIRST.test` when `inpage` is absent; and `photoreal.ts:289` runs `JSON.stringify(applied())` every frame.
3. **Persistence `DELTAS` evicts freshly eaten berry/clover patches first, crashes every second on any unknown saved delta kind, and makes rain-accelerated tracks jump backwards in age when rain stops**: `deltas.ts:61,68` sorts cap evictions by `strength(a) * a.m`, where a completely grazed patch has `m = 0` (`strength = 1.0`) → `1.0 × 0 = 0`, making brand-new eaten patches the #1 first entry dropped at cap `1500` (instantly respawning them to 100%); `loadDeltas()` (`deltas.ts:80`) accepts unvalidated JSON arrays so any unknown/retired `kind` causes `prune()` (`deltas.ts:74`, called every 1 s in `main.ts:315`) to throw `TypeError: Cannot read properties of undefined (reading 'until')` and abort all autosaves; `strength()` (`deltas.ts:48–49`) multiplies total historical age `(now - d.t)` by instantaneous `wet = 4`, dropping a 2-day dry track `54.6×` during a 1-minute shower and rejuvenating it `54.6×` when rain stops; and `main.ts:312–314` records footprints on a `1000 ms` `setInterval` (Rule 5 violation) with hardcoded `'canid'` footing even when playing as deer/bear/hare.
4. **`interest.ts` `estSlice` locks multi-slice scheduling to 1 slice/frame after a single GC pause (with no exception guard), and `snapshot.ts` wipes the entire 60 s rewind ring on resume while omitting `needs`/`modes`/`worldClock`**: A single `40 ms` GC spike in `runJobs()` (`src/core/interest.ts:68,74`) inflates `st.estSlice` to `40 ms` and decays it by `×0.9` only when run, deferring `9 / 9` subsequent `0.05 ms` frames (`10 / 30` steps completed in 10 frames vs `30 / 30`) and leaving any throwing generator stuck in `jobs` forever; `createSnapshotHub.sample()` (`src/core/snapshot.ts:35`) clears the entire ring whenever `time < last` (so rewinding from snapshot 50 → 25 and resuming 1 tick wipes the buffer to size `1`), while `main.ts:236–242` never registers `needs`, `modes`, or `worldClock` so rewound `simTime` permanently diverges from thirst/stamina/sky clock.
