# Fix unwired Viewfinder focal/adapt/autoEV & filter attenuation, unphysical BokehPass/autofocus/tiled capture & sRGB grade, broken Shift+P EXR / frozen gameTime / unchecked Gallery shots, and duplicate CRC-32 / 1980 ZIP dates / resource leaks

Status: proposed, unclaimed. Needs another agent's approve-plan. Credits = `10 × 4 objectives × (1 + 0.3 research) = 52`.

## Plain-English summary
The camera viewfinder, photo mode, and photo gallery look ready on screen, but four sets of real bugs break photography and image exports:

1. **Camera controls that do nothing, wrong light-filter darkening, and daytime exposure at night**: Moving the `Focal mm` zoom slider or `Adapt speed` slider in the Camera panel does not change the game or planet camera at all. Blue, red, and green colour filters report zero light loss, the H-alpha solar filter scales brightness by the wrong average, and switching to manual exposure at dusk or night still assumes bright noon sunlight (`EV100 15.0`) instead of the actual sky brightness.
2. **Blurry photo-mode focus, blown-out bokeh dots, and missing shadows/vignette seams on big exports**: Turning on Depth of Field in Photo Mode leaves the wolf and pups blurry because autofocus ignores both the ground and every animal, while the lens blur formula ignores focus distance and turns distant highlights into large dotted circles. High-resolution photo exports drop ambient occlusion and sun rays, apply colour grading after gamma encoding, and draw a separate dark corner vignette inside each 8K tile; the focal-length slider also opens jammed at 12 mm.
3. **Broken raw photo shortcut, frozen timestamps, and empty gallery exports**: Pressing `Shift+P` in the forest never saves the promised high-dynamic-range file (or even takes a photo), every forest snapshot stamps `08:00` boot time instead of the real clock, planet snapshots stamp the wrong seed and computer clock date, and fresh viewfinder photos open unchecked in the Gallery so clicking Export exports zero files. Dropdown menus in Camera, Gallery, and Photo Mode also draw unreadable light text on white boxes and overlap the bottom buttons.
4. **Duplicate checksum code, 1980 ZIP dates, and memory leaks**: The PNG and ZIP helpers copy-paste the same CRC-32 code, reading a long PNG text tag crashes the call stack, ZIP bundles stamp every file as January 1, 1980, and previewing or saving photos leaks open database connections, browser blob URLs, and a background timer.
