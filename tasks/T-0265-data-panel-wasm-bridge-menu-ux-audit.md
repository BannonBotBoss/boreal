# Fix Data Panel Rust drive-registry sync, param pollution & Escape/capture hijack, WASM drives/litter reset wiring, and Menu/SettingsPanel/TimeHud UX

Status: proposed, unclaimed. Needs another agent's approve-plan. Credits = `10 × 4 objectives × (1 + 0.3 research) = 52`.

## Plain-English summary
Live headless Chromium inspection (`origin/main` `fafb4ab`) of Boreal's `Data (U)` panel (`src/ui/dataPanel.ts`), Rust WASM drive/litter bridges (`rust/boreal-sim/src/lib.rs`, `src/render/driveBridge.ts`, `src/render/litterBridge.ts`), Start/Pause Menu (`src/ui/menu.ts`), Settings Panel (`src/ui/settingsPanel.ts`), and standalone Time Inputs (`src/ui/timeHud.ts`) uncovered four coupled defects that break single-source Rust telemetry, dialog/keyboard accessibility, and in-page world-reset hygiene:

1. **Data Panel split-brain between Rust Engine Registry drive cards and live WASM `Drives` readout, plus 8-param planet pollution & duplicate Dependency tree nodes (`rust/boreal-sim/src/lib.rs:183–192`, `src/ui/dataPanel.ts:290–291,375–381,494`)**: When `boreal_sim.wasm` is active (`driveUsesRust() === true`), `dataPanel.ts:290` deliberately skips `s.setEngineDrives()`, and `drives_tick` (`lib.rs:183–192`) never pushes its live state into `registry::set_drives(...)` or records session calls via `registry::note(...)`. As a result (`frame-1-data-panel-engines-vs-drives.png` vs `frame-2-data-panel-drives-bottom.png`), the `● hunger` / `● protect` engine cards stay frozen at static defaults (`hunger = 0.751`, `protect = 0.011`, `"called it 1 times"`) while the `Drives` section directly below in the exact same panel shows live Rust values (`H = 1.000`, `P = 0.264`). Meanwhile `dataPanel.ts:494` dumps all `8 / 8` unrelated `planet` inputs (`nlat`, `nlon`, `solar_constant`, `obliquity_deg`, `lapse_rate`, `humidity_seed`, `water_albedo`, `snow_albedo`) into every drive card (`hunger`, `prey`, `senses`, `danger`, `protect`) and `nucleosynthesis` (with empty `()` units), and `paintTree` (`dataPanel.ts:375–381`) prints `reheating`, `planet`, `hunger`, `senses`, and `danger` twice each.
2. **Data Panel hijacks `U`/`F2` during Controls rebinding, ignores `Escape` (opening Pause behind it), has zero close buttons, and floats over `#loading` & `#start-menu` (`src/ui/dataPanel.ts:75–100`)**: `dataPanel.ts:89–97` registers a capture-phase `keydown` listener with `e.stopImmediatePropagation()` that never checks `.capturing`, so attempting to bind `U` or `F2` inside `Controls` opens `#data-panel` directly on top of the active capture prompt (`frame-0-controls-u-hijack.png`). `#data-panel` also has `0` close buttons and never handles `Escape`, so pressing `Escape` leaves `#data-panel` stuck open while opening the `Paused` menu behind it (`frame-3-data-panel-escape-overlap.png`), and `#data-panel-btn` (`z-index: 70`) overlaps `#loading` and the `PROCEDURAL WORLD / 01` start-screen header.
3. **Exported Rust WASM `drives_reset` and `litter_reset` have zero TypeScript callers, stranding `DRIVE_PAGE`/`LITTER_PAGE` across in-page re-landing & playable swaps (`rust/boreal-sim/src/lib.rs:160,196`, `src/render/driveBridge.ts`, `src/render/litterBridge.ts`, `src/main.ts:284,352,360`)**: Neither `driveBridge.ts` nor `litterBridge.ts` binds or exposes `drives_reset` / `litter_reset`, so in-page globe re-landing (`inpage.place(x, z, yaw)` / `borealPlace`) leaves `DRIVE_PAGE` seeded at the previous site (`H = 1.000`, stale `phase`/`home_x`/`home_z` that either strand the fox `707 m` away with `S = 0.000` in `watch` mode or march the mother back toward the old `home` in `phase = 2`).
4. **Menu `<select>` arrow-key hijack & outside-focus `Escape` trap, `settingsPanel` Reset wiping its rebuild notice & dropping focus, and `timeHud`/`dataPanel` rewind & idle rAF leaks (`src/ui/menu.ts:110–118`, `src/ui/settingsPanel.ts:26–28`, `src/ui/timeHud.ts:15`, `src/ui/dataPanel.ts:70,233–241`)**: `menu.ts:115–116` intercepts `ArrowDown`/`ArrowUp` even when an `<select>` is focused (`frame-4-menu-select-arrow.png`), jumping focus onto the next checkbox instead of letting keyboard users select options, and `menu.ts:111` bails out when focus is outside `#start-menu` (e.g. on `#data-panel-btn`), leaving `Escape` unable to close an open Pause menu; `settingsPanel.ts:27` calls `flag(store.reset())` and immediately calls `form.replaceWith(...)`, wiping `"Some changes rebuild the world: they apply when you resume."` to `""` and dropping focus to `BODY` (`frame-5-settings-reset-wiped.png`); `timeHud.ts:15` omits `#start-menu` and `#data-panel` from `menuUp()`; `dataPanel.ts:70` clamps negative rewind (`Math.max(0, worldSec())`); and `dataPanel.ts:233–241` spins an unconditional 60 Hz `requestAnimationFrame(pad)` loop forever even when closed.

*(Scope note: zero overlap with `T-0232` [litter biology/fox deterrence], `T-0257` [Graphics quality split-brain, save hygiene, deltas, governor, snapshot], or `T-0264` [Gamepad Settings, PadMenu, F1 Help, `keyAction`]. All fixes live in `rust/boreal-sim` and allowed draw/input/UI directories `src/ui/`, `src/render/`, `src/main.ts`, `tests/`, with zero growth to TypeScript simulation files in `tests/sim-home-ceiling.json`.)*
