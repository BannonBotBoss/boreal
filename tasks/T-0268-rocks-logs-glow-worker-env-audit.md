## Plain-English summary
Live headless Chromium inspection (`origin/main` `041ba10`, `1280×720`, SwiftShader, `0` console errors) and calibrated Rule 11 measurements across Boreal's chunk batching & rock/log shaders (`src/render/batch.ts`, `src/render/geometry.ts`, `src/render/rocks.ts`), target silhouette outline pass (`src/render/targetGlow.ts`, `src/render/buildScene.ts`), terrain WebWorker pool (`src/render/terrainPool.ts`), and shared render environment uniforms (`src/render/environment.ts`) uncovered four coupled rendering/infrastructure defects — all strictly in allowed `src/render/` draw files with zero overlap with `T-0224`..`T-0267` and zero growth to `tests/sim-home-ceiling.json`:

1. **Winter snowpack ignores `ROCK_MATERIAL` boulders & `LOG_MATERIAL` fallen logs (`0/2` materials snow-coupled), and `ChunkBatch.cover(key, 0)` stamps singular `det = 0` scale matrices onto visible instances (`src/render/batch.ts:19–51,149–154`, `src/render/geometry.ts:20–56`, `src/render/buildScene.ts:528`)**:
   - In deep January snowpack (`frame-0-winter-rocks-logs.png`, `2026-01-19 12:13`, HUD `Snow 34.1 cm`, `snowOnGroundCm(34) = 34 cm`), neither `ROCK_MATERIAL` (`batch.ts:19–51`) nor `LOG_MATERIAL` (`geometry.ts:20–56`) reads a winter snowpack uniform (`rockSnow = false`, `logSnow = false`, `0/2` materials). Every Canadian Shield granite whaleback boulder displays bright summer green feathermoss (`horizontal top linear luma = 0.112` vs `0.75–0.85` linear snow cap, Wiscombe & Warren 1980; Sturm et al. 1995) and every fallen spruce log displays dry brown bark — visually identical to mid-August summer (`frame-1-summer-slope-rocks.png`, `Snow 0.0 cm`).
   - In `batch.ts:149–154`, `cover(key, 0)` (called every frame by `buildScene.ts:528` `landingHidesCover` and on depleted forage) writes `Vector3(0, 0, 0)` (`det(matrixAtZero) = 0.000000`, singular `batchingNormalMatrix` -> `NaN` normals) while keeping `bm.getVisibleAt(0) === true`, instead of hiding the instance via `bm.setVisibleAt(slot.inst, false)`.
2. **`seatRock` hoists small cobbles (`d <= 0.22 m`) `+10.23 cm` into mid-air on `25°` slopes, `bigTier` boulders miss `22.4×` steeper carved river-bank gradients, and `rocks.ts` wastes `DoubleSide` + an unused `16×16` `NearestFilter` `DataTexture` (`src/render/rocks.ts:6–14,52–62,79,84–100`)**:
   - `seatRock` (`rocks.ts:97,99`) computes `lift = Math.max(lift, heightAt(e.x + v.x, e.z + v.z) - h0 - v.y)` for `d <= 0.22 m`, lifting the rock until its highest uphill vertex sits on top of the slope (`0%` basal embedding). On a `25°` boreal hillslope, a `22.0 cm` cobble's downhill underside hovers **`+10.23 cm` (`46.5%` of its diameter) in mid-air** (and across `n = 25` live cobbles in chunks `[-1..1]²` on `>= 8°` slopes, up to **`+7.24 cm` at `10.0°`**), violating natural `20–40%` hillslope cobble embedment (`<= 0 cm` downhill air gap; Wentworth 1922; Carson 1977).
   - For `bigTier` boulders (`size >= 9`, `rocks.ts:53–56`), slope shearing samples raw `elevationGrid(STAGES.seed)` instead of `heightAt(...)`, completely ignoring staged river-bank carving (`gridGrad = 0.051` vs true `heightAt` `trueGrad = 1.141`, **`err = 1.121`**, `22.4×` error) and remote landing terrain gradients.
   - `rocks.ts:6–14,79` sets `side: THREE.DoubleSide` on closed icospheres (`2×` rasterization waste) and attaches a pixelated `16×16` `NearestFilter` `DataTexture` that `batch.ts:92` discards for every batched rock (leaving only pushed rock LODs showing a `16×16` checkerboard).
3. **`TargetGlow` produces `0` mirrored meshes on Meshy-skinned deer, freezes `SkinnedMesh` outlines in static bind-pose, leaks every static proxy material, and blazes at full daytime brightness at midnight (`src/render/targetGlow.ts:23–69`, `src/render/buildScene.ts:603–613`)**:
   - `buildScene.ts:606` passes `animal.rig.root` to `glow.set(...)`, which `animalPicture.ts` hides (`visible = false`) whenever the Meshy GLB skin is active. Because `mirrorMeshes` (`targetGlow.ts:26`) uses `source.traverseVisible`, it collects **`0` meshes** (`meshyHiddenRigFollow = 0`), producing zero silhouette highlight on Meshy deer.
   - On visible `THREE.SkinnedMesh` targets, `targetGlow.ts:28` creates a plain `new THREE.Mesh(m.geometry, material)` (`proxyIsSkinnedMesh = false`), discarding skeleton skinning so the outline stays frozen in static bind-pose instead of tracking live limb animation.
   - For batched static targets (`buildScene.ts:612` `generateGeometry(e, 0)`), `targetGlow.ts:51` overwrites `m.material = material` without disposing the freshly allocated source materials and `clear()` (`line 41`) only disposes geometries (`staticMatsDisposed = 0`, `geos = 1`) — leaking GPU materials on every target change.
   - `targetGlow.ts:62–68` blends additive `#ffd89a` (`edgeStrength = 1.3`) onto the post-tonemapped canvas with **zero nocturnal illuminance scaling** (`nightAttenuation = false`), staying full daytime intensity in `0.001 lx` winter midnight (`frame-3-midnight-winter-rocks.png`).
4. **`terrainPool` worker `onerror` permanently deadlocks queued chunks on `WAIT` & `closeTerrainWorkers` prevents clean respawn, while `environment.ts` has `0` ground callers, `0` shader uniform consumers, and inverted austral-winter phenology (`src/render/terrainPool.ts:24–68`, `src/render/environment.ts:45–67`)**:
   - When a terrain WebWorker hits `onerror` (`terrainPool.ts:30`), it sets `broken = true` and fails only `s.busy`, leaving **`0 / 3` queued chunk handles failed** (so every queued `chunkTerrainJob` yields `WAIT` indefinitely), leaving `terminatedOnError = false` instead of calling `failTerrainWorkers(...)`. Meanwhile `warmTerrainWorkers()` (`line 46–47`) advances `warmKey` while skipping busy slots, and `closeTerrainWorkers()` (`line 68`) leaves `broken = true` / stale `warmKey` (`respawnAfterClose = false`) and un-failed handles.
   - `src/render/environment.ts` (`T-0089`, issues `#110`/`#111`) has **`0` live callers of `fillEnvironmentGround`** (`ENV.source === null` throughout ground play) and **`0` live shader consumers of `ENV_UNIFORMS`** (only `globeView.ts` calls `fillEnvironmentGlobe`), while `finish()` (`line 47`) calls `phenology(day)` without a Southern Hemisphere `+182.5 d` phase shift — reporting midsummer full-leaf `{ colour: 0, off: 0 }` on June 21 (`day = 172`) at `-45°S` austral winter solstice (`off = 1.0`).
