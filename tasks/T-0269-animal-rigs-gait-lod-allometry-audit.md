1. **`proceduralAnimal.ts` scrambles `[LF, LH, RF, RH]` leg indices onto `[RF, LF, RH, LH]` (swapping front & hind knees!) and inflates an unlit `MeshBasicMaterial` Red Fox by `1.7×`**: `quadrupedPose` (`src/core/animalBehavior.ts:123, 151, 202`) outputs `legs[]` and `knees[]` strictly in `[LF, LH, RF, RH]` order (front legs at indices `0, 2`; hind legs at `1, 3`). Both `mammal()` (`src/render/proceduralAnimal.ts:61`) and `redFox()` (`src/render/proceduralAnimal.ts:147`) instead lay hips in `spots = [[1, -1], [-1, -1], [1, 1], [-1, 1]]` (`[RF, LF, RH, LH]` — **`0 / 4` exact quadrant matches**), feeding the Left-Front leg (`i = 1`) hind-leg phase and hind knee bend and the Right-Hind leg (`i = 2`) front-leg phase and front knee bend!
