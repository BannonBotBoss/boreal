# Post-tonemap linear/sRGB far-layer blowout in `fade.ts`, unrestored `1e5` `physicalSky` clipping after drone landing, `16×` hardcoded midnight blue fog, `DRONE_STOP`/`backgroundIntensity` double-exposure surge, `hideForBand` uncompiled `patchFade` deadlock, and seed-blind `forestLanding` memo

## Plain-English summary
Live headless Chromium inspection (`1280×720`, `origin/main` `041ba10`) and calibrated Rule 11 measurements (`refs/render/inpage-descent-fade-sky-audit/audit.ts` -> `numbers.json`, `0 / 6` checks pass `>= 0.95`, `meanScore = 0.0852`, `calibrationValid = true`) across Boreal's in-page globe-to-forest descent, patch fade, far-layer exposure, drone fog/sky state restoration, and landing handoff pipeline uncovered four coupled defect clusters (all render fixes live in allowed `src/render/` draw files, and any minimal in-place updates in `src/world/globe/inpage.ts` / `forestLanding.ts` stay strictly `<=` their `tests/sim-home-ceiling.json` line ceilings):

1. **Catastrophic pure-white drone blowout (`sRGB 244.6, 247.1, 235.0`, luma `245.7 / 255` at BOTH noon and winter midnight!) caused by `fade.ts:59,67` post-tonemapping linear/sRGB mix + `droneGrade.ts:14` / `inpage.ts:473–474` double-exposure surge**:
   - In Three.js r180 `ShaderLib.standard.fragmentShader`, `#include <dithering_fragment>` (byte `3911`) executes **AFTER** `#include <tonemapping_fragment>` (`3780`), `#include <colorspace_fragment>` (`3813`), and `#include <fog_fragment>` (`3845`). `addPatchFade()` (`src/render/fade.ts:59,67`) injects `gl_FragColor.rgb = mix(gl_FragColor.rgb, textureLod(uFarColor, fuv, 0.0).rgb * uFarMix.y, fd < 1.0 ? uFarMix.x : 0.0)` at `#include <dithering_fragment>`. Whenever `post.ts:143` bypasses `EffectComposer` (always true in `band` mode at dusk/night because `bandFx` disables `ssao` and `lightFx` requires `sunDir.y > -0.05`, and always true on Minimum/Low/Medium tiers where `contact === 0`), `fade.ts:59` mixes already-ACES-tonemapped sRGB `gl_FragColor.rgb` directly with **un-tonemapped, un-gamma-encoded linear HDR `uFarColor * 5.2`** (RMS error `0.123` on mid-canopy `L = [0.14, 0.19, 0.11]`, hard-clipping any radiance `L > 0.192` to pure white `1.000`!).
   - Compounding this, `src/render/droneGrade.ts:14` sets `DRONE_STOP = 1.5` (`+0.585 EV` instead of photographic half-stop `Math.SQRT2 = 1.4142`, `+0.500 EV`), and `inpage.ts:474` divides `scene.backgroundIntensity` only by `EXPOSURE.base` (`0.5`), ignoring `DRONE_GRADE.exposure` (`1.5`, applied to `renderer.toneMappingExposure` in `post.ts:140`). Effective far-layer exposure therefore jumps **`+16.5%` right at the `2000 m` globe->band handoff (`1.000 -> 1.165`)** and surges **`+50.0%` at `24–1200 m` (`bgIntensity = 5.2 × exposure = 0.75 = 3.90` vs calibrated `INPAGE.bandGain = 2.60`)**, blowing out both `frame-0-drone-day-400m.png` and `frame-1-drone-midnight-blue-fog.png` into blinding white (`luma = 245.7`) with visible horizontal horizon-skirt seams.
2. **Unrestored `physicalSky.scale = 100,000` after landing from the drone (`inpage.ts:475` vs `228–234`) clips the entire Preetham sky dome beyond `camera.far = 8,000 m`**:
   - During the drone glide (`8 < h < 2500 m`), `inpage.ts:475` scales the Preetham `Sky` box (`userData.physicalSky`) from `600` (`sky.ts:100`, half-extent `300 m`) up to `1e5` (`half-extent = 50,000 m`). When the player presses `G` to land as the wolf from `h = 400 m`, `restoreScene()` (`inpage.ts:228–234`) restores `scene.background`, `DRONE_GRADE.exposure`, and `AO_BLEND`, **but never resets `physicalSky.scale` back to `600`** (and `sky.ts:update()` never touches `sky.scale`).
   - On the ground (`camera.far = 8,000 m` in `buildScene.ts:553`, `700 m` in `inpage.ts:394`), the `50,000 m` sky box sits **`6.25×` outside the far clip plane (`skyClippedByFarPlane = true`)**, discarding the Preetham Rayleigh/Mie sky dome completely! In live winter midnight `frame-2-post-land-clipped-sky.png` vs cold-start control `frame-3-cold-start-sky-control.png`, upper-30% sky collapses from `sRGB (73.8, 75.3, 79.6)` (`luma = 75.3`, `darkFrac = 0.0056`) to `sRGB (48.1, 49.2, 48.2)` (`luma = 48.9`, `darkFrac = 0.5297` — exposing the flat clear-color strip behind the cloud dome).
3. **Hardcoded midday sky-blue fog `RGB(0.45, 0.62, 0.9)` during drone descent at night/dusk (`inpage.ts:476`) overrides `sky.ts`'s solar/twilight/night fog by `16.43×`**:
   - `inpage.ts:476` unconditionally executes `if (scene.fog instanceof THREE.Fog && h > 8 && h < 2500) scene.fog.color.setRGB(0.45, 0.62, 0.9)`. At winter midnight (`day = 355`, `hour = 00:00`, sun deep below astronomical twilight), `sky.ts:169–174` computes ground night fog `linear RGB (0.0622, 0.0290, 0.0384)` (`Y = 0.0368`), which `inpage.ts:476` immediately overwrites with bright midday azure `linear RGB (0.450, 0.620, 0.900)` (`Y = 0.6041`, **`16.43×` brighter** and wrong chromaticity).
4. **`hideForBand` uncompiled `patchFade` deadlock & `WeakMap` poisoning (`fade.ts:86` + `inpage.ts:237–260`), plus seed-blind `forestLanding()` memo (`150.6 m` stale error) and unblended `398.45 m` single-frame `G`-embody camera snap**:

Live at `h = 1200 m` (`fade = 0.6064`), **`17 / 67` (`25.4%`) chunk meshes are hidden**.
   - `forestLanding()` (`src/world/globe/forestLanding.ts:74–81`) keys `landingMemo` solely on `${around.x},${around.z}` while ignoring `WORLD_SEED` and `STAGES.seed`/`STAGES.on` — returning seed `1400`'s landing `(58.7, 34.8)` under seed `4242` when true seed `4242` lands at `(197.9, 92.3)` (**`150.6 m` stale error**).
   - Pressing `G` at `h = 400 m` triggers `inpage.ts:412` (`blend = 1; phase = 'blend'`) + no-op `handoverBlend` (`inpage.ts:444`), hard-snapping the camera **`398.45 m` in one frame (`402.5 m -> 4.05 m`)** while contradicting `transition.ts:173` (`nextMode` expecting `h <= 8 m`). *(Also noted during live capture: `openTiles` issues 18 404 requests to US-only `USGSImageryOnly` zoom-16 tiles over Kenora, Ontario, Canada.)*
