# Fix flat-top globe Lambertian shading (`N·L=1` above `14.5°`), floating-origin sliding `vAerial`/`detN`/`snoise`/`uEra` & `±19 m` fake lowland lakes, equatorial `vAerial.xz` barcode streaking, half-pixel NW `coarseRelief`/`openTiles` shift & antimeridian/`inConus` bugs, and `80.9%` `lakeMask` river dropout

Status: proposed, unclaimed. Needs another agent's approve-plan. Credits = `10 × 4 objectives × (1 + 0.3 research) = 52`.

## Plain-English summary
Live headless Chromium inspection (`origin/main` `d674940`, `1280×720`, SwiftShader, `0` console/page errors) and calibrated Rule 11 verification across Boreal's globe tile shader, aerial exemplar coordinate projection, zoom-0 coarse DEM relief, open-tile sampling/bounds, and home shoreline mask (`src/world/globe/tile.ts`, `src/world/globe/coarseRelief.ts`, `src/world/globe/openTiles.ts`, `src/world/globe/lakeMask.ts`) uncovered four coupled defects — with zero overlap with `T-0205` (`biomeGrade` latitude ramp), `T-0245` (`uCrownFloor` midnight override / `aerialExemplar` `NoColorSpace` / `planet.home` Kazakhstan longitude), or `T-0247` (hydroclimate / `planetLayers` / `localClouds` / `plates` / `axisView`):

1. **Flat-top `smoothstep(-0.08, 0.25, N·L)` in `tile.ts:564–566` saturates `dotNL = 1.0000` across `75%` of the sunlit Earth (`h_sun >= 14.48°`), erasing Lambertian cosine limb darkening and zeroing sunlit mountain/ridge shading**:
   - Replacing `saturate(dot(geometryNormal, directLight.direction))` with `smoothstep(-0.08, 0.25, N·L)` clamps direct irradiance to **`1.0000`** everywhere `N·L >= 0.25`: at `15°` low sun (`sin 15° = 0.2588`), the globe receives **`3.86×` Lambert's cosine law**; at `30°` sun (`sin 30° = 0.500`), slope shading sensitivity `d(dotNL)/d(N·L) = 0.0000` (all sunlit topographic relief shading disappears); and at geometric sunset (`N·L = 0`), direct sunlight remains `14.78%` of full noon irradiance down to `-4.58°` below the horizon (`5.5×` deeper than the `0.83°` solar disc + atmospheric refraction penumbra). In `frame-0-orbit-flat-lambert.png`, the sunlit gibbous Earth jumps abruptly from the terminator into a flat bright plateau (`f70 = 187.90`, `f85 = 193.98` right at the limb > `f50 = 106.40` at center) with zero cosine limb falloff.

2. **Floating-origin sign inversion (`vAerial = vDetW - uMorphCam = world - 2*cam`), `20` camera-relative `detN(vDetW / ...)` calls, `3` camera-relative ocean-chop `gd_snoise(vDetW)` calls, camera-relative early-Earth `normalize(vDetW)`, and `±19 m` `detN(vDetW/180)` punching fake lakes into lowland forest (`src/world/globe/tile.ts:348–349,373–377,407–455,484–488,496,531–533`)**:
   - Because `globeView.ts:518` renders tile meshes camera-relative (`mesh.position = centre - camPos`, `camera.position = 0`), `vDetW = (modelMatrix * vec4(transformed, 1.0)).xyz = world - camPos`. While `MORPH_GLSL` (`tile.ts:259`) properly adds `vertW = vertCam + uMorphCam` and `uDetail` (`tile.ts:475`) adds CPU modular offsets `uDetO0..2`, **`tile.ts:349` subtracts `uMorphCam` (`vAerial = vDetW - uMorphCam = world - 2*cam`)**, driving Deliot–Heitz NAIP aerial photography backwards at `2×` camera velocity relative to ground, while **`20` `detN(vDetW / ...)` calls**, **`3` `gd_snoise(vDetW * ...)` ocean-chop calls**, and **`uEra`'s `normalize(vDetW)`** omit the camera world offset completely.
   - Live `+160 m` horizontal pan at `alt = 2,600 m` (`frame-1-aerial-pan-t0.png` -> `frame-1-aerial-pan-t1.png`, `recording.webm`, `expectedWorldDxPx = -53.48 px`): green deciduous forest stands stay locked to the camera lens (`measuredStandPatternDxPx = -2 px`, **`groundRelativeSlipPx = 51.48 px = 96.3% slip`**, `standCorrAtZeroPx = 0.3501`). Worse, `tile.ts:376–377` applies `±19.0 m` camera-relative `detN(vDetW / 180)` + `detN(vDetW / 55)` whenever `abs(F) < 6.0 m` (`shoreGate = 1.0` across nearly all gentle Kenora lowland forest, **even when `hm = 1` `lakeMask` is active!**), carving dozens of spurious lakes directly into dry forest (`frame-1-aerial-pan-t1.png`).

3. **Equatorial-plane `uv = vAerial.xz / uExMetres` projection (`tile.ts:407`) collapses North–South UVs to zero at the Equator (`4.36×` vertical barcode streaking) and foreshortens mid-latitudes (`1.44×` at `52°N`)**:
   - Projecting ECEF `(x, y, z)` onto `.xz` aligns the texture with the equatorial plane rather than the local tangent plane. At `0°N, -62°W` (Amazon rainforest nadir, `alt = 2,600 m`, `frame-2-equator-xz-streaking.png`), `d(vAerial.xz)/d(north) = (0, 0)`, smearing the entire tropical rainforest into infinite vertical barcode curtains (`mean |dI/dx| = 0.7982` vs `|dI/dy| = 0.1830`, **`anisotropyRatio = 4.3622×`**, `isotropyScore = 0.2292` vs isotropic `ref-1-equatorial-canopy-nadir.jpg`), while boreal `52°N` exhibits `1.4361×` directional stretch and `|vAerial| ≈ 12.74 × 10^6 m` loses sub-metre float32 precision.

4. **Half-pixel NW Web-Mercator registration shift in `coarseRelief.ts:1501–1514` (`78.3 km` at equator, up to `3,165.5 m` error) & `openTiles.ts:114–142` (`0.5 px` shift + broken `±180°` antimeridian wrap + `inConus` Canada/Mexico false positives), plus `80.88%` sub-texel river dropout & non-periodic wrap seam in `lakeMask.ts:25–53` + `tile.ts:360`**:
   - Both `coarseMetresLinear` (`coarseRelief.ts:1506–1507`) and `openTiles.ts:116–119` (`sampleGrid`) compute bilinear indices via `Math.floor(x)` instead of `Math.floor(x - 0.5)` (pixel centers sit at `ix + 0.5, iy + 0.5`). Consequently, `coarseMetresLinear` disagrees with `coarseMetres` at **`3,771 / 3,776` (`99.87%`)** exact pixel centers (`meanAbsErr = 162.43 m`, **`maxErr = 3,165.5 m`**) while agreeing at `100%` of pixel corners (`cornerDiffs = 0`), shifting the entire interpolated Earth DEM **`0.5` pixel (`78.3 km` at equator, `50.6 km` at `50°N`) NW**.
   - In `openTiles.ts`, `sampleGrid` shifts bilinear DEM/photo lookups `0.5 px` NW (`505.0 m` vs `500.0 m` at exact pixel center `50.5`), fails to wrap `tx` modulo `2^z` across the `±180°` antimeridian (`sampleDem(-16.8, 179.999) = 100.0 m` clamped instead of blending to `227.2 m`, and `tilesAround:268` requests out-of-range `tx = -1 / 2^z` 404 tiles), and `inConus:40–42` (`lat <= 50, -125 <= lon <= -66`) falsely classifies **`7 / 7`** tested Canadian/Mexican cities (**Kenora ON**, Winnipeg MB, Vancouver BC, Toronto ON, Ottawa ON, Montreal QC, Monterrey MX) as CONUS, firing wasted z16 USGS Imagery Only fetches.
   - In `lakeMask.ts`, `1024×1024` over `7,680 m` (`7.5 m/texel`) undersamples Strahler order 1–2 channels (Bieger et al. 2015 width `3.3–7.2 m`), dropping **`165 / 204` (`80.88%`)** of true river channel points (`39 / 204 = 19.12%` retained; `frame-3-home-shore-and-rivers.png` vs `ref-2-boreal-shield-lakes-streams-nadir.jpg`), while `setLakeMask`'s `RepeatWrapping` produces a **`2.75 m` height discontinuity** across the non-periodic wrap edge when `staged = false`, and `tile.ts:360` snaps shorelines with binary `step(0.5, vLoc.z)` instead of a smooth blend.

## Objectives
- [ ] **Restore physical Lambertian cosine shading & narrow solar-disc penumbra on globe tiles (`src/world/globe/tile.ts`, shader helper in `src/render/`)**: Replace `float dotNL = smoothstep(-0.08, 0.25, dot(geometryNormal, directLight.direction))` (`tile.ts:564–566`) with physically accurate cosine falloff plus a narrow `~0.83°` solar-disc + atmospheric-refraction penumbra near `N·L ≈ 0` (e.g., `max(0.0, N·L)` smoothly softened only inside `[-0.015, +0.025]`, preserving `dotNL = N·L` and `d(dotNL)/d(N·L) = 1.0` for all sunlit elevations `> 2°`) so orbital limb darkening and sunlit mountain/ridge shading match Lambert's cosine law.
- [ ] **Anchor all globe tile UVs, procedural noise, ocean chop, & early-Earth normals to world coordinates with isotropic tangent/triplanar projection and guard `shoreGate` off `lakeMask` (`src/world/globe/tile.ts`, helper in `src/render/`)**: Fix the floating-origin sign inversion in `tile.ts:349` (using double-precision CPU modular camera offsets like `uDetO0` instead of `vDetW - uMorphCam`) and pass world-anchored modular coordinates to all `20` `detN(...)` calls, `3` ocean-chop `gd_snoise(...)` calls, and `uEra`'s planet-centric `normalize(vDetW + uMorphCam)` so `0%` texture/stand/wave slip occurs during horizontal pans; replace equatorial `vAerial.xz` (`tile.ts:407`) with local tangent-plane or cube-face/triplanar UVs so equatorial rainforests (`0°N`) render isotropically (`|dI/dx|/|dI/dy| ≈ 1.0`); and gate `tile.ts:376–377` shoreline wander by `(1.0 - hm)` and limit amplitude so it never overwrites `lakeMask` or carves fake lakes into dry lowland forest.
- [ ] **Fix half-pixel Web-Mercator registration in `coarseRelief` & `openTiles`, wrap `±180°` antimeridian tiles, and narrow `inConus` to true US coverage (`src/world/globe/coarseRelief.ts`, `src/world/globe/openTiles.ts`, moving simulation math to `rust/boreal-sim` to keep ceilinged TS files non-growing)**: Subtract `0.5` before `Math.floor` in `coarseMetresLinear` (`coarseRelief.ts:1506–1507`) and `openTiles.ts:116–119` (`sampleGrid`) so bilinear interpolation reproduces exact grid values at pixel centers `(ix + 0.5, iy + 0.5)` (`0.0 m` error, eliminating the `78.3 km` equator NW shift); wrap `tx` modulo `2^z` in both `sampleGrid:121` and `tilesAround:268` (clamping `ty` to `[0, 2^z - 1]`) so `±180°` antimeridian sampling blends seamlessly without 404 holes; and replace `inConus`'s `lat <= 50` rectangle (`openTiles.ts:40–42`) with a border-accurate CONUS mask excluding Canada (Kenora, Winnipeg, Vancouver, Toronto, Ottawa, Montreal) and Mexico.
- [ ] **Preserve narrow Strahler order 1–2 rivers in `lakeMask`, eliminate non-periodic wrap seams, and smooth the home boundary blend (`src/world/globe/lakeMask.ts`, `src/world/globe/tile.ts`, hydrology helper in `rust/boreal-sim` / `src/render/`)**: Ensure `buildLakeMask` preserves continuous order 1–2 stream channels (`>= 95%` channel retention via higher resolution, signed channel distance field, or analytic river overlay) instead of dropping `80.88%` at `7.5 m/texel`; use `THREE.ClampToEdgeWrapping` (instead of `RepeatWrapping`) when `staged = false` so non-periodic layered terrain has zero `2.75 m` wrap-edge discontinuity; and replace hard binary `step(0.5, vLoc.z)` (`tile.ts:360`) with a smooth feather across the home blend margin.
