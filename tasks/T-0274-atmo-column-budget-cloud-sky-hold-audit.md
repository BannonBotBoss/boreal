## Plain-English summary

1. **Negative shortwave surface absorption (`-51.08 W/m²`) & reflection (`-0.44 W/m²`) under deep clouds, `+103.51 W/m²` phantom absorption at `tau = 0.001`, and `+79.5%` excess solar absorption over cloudy snow (`rust/boreal-sim/src/column_budget.rs:27–39`)**:
   - `column_budget::column_with` applies a fixed tau-independent cloud absorption `ac = cloud * CLOUD_ABS * s2` (`CLOUD_ABS = 0.12`, line `31`) alongside two-stream reflection `rc = cloud * cloud_albedo(tau) * s2` (line `30`). For deep cumulonimbus (`cloud = 1, tau = 120, cosZ = 0.8`), `R_c + A_c = 0.940 + 0.120 = 1.060 > 1.0`, making `s3 = s2 - rc - ac` **negative** and returning unphysical **negative surface solar absorption (`absSurf = -51.08 W/m²`) and negative reflection (`reflSurf = -0.44 W/m²`)**. Conversely, at an optically empty cloud (`cloud = 1, tau = 0.001`), `ac` still absorbs **`+103.51 W/m²` (`+58.1%` jump in `absAtm` from `178.16` to `281.67 W/m²`)** even though cloud absorption must vanish as `tau -> 0` (Stephens 1978), and `rayleigh_refl(mu) * ray` (line `27`) is unclamped above `1.0` for dense atmospheres.
   - On the upward surface-to-cloud bounce (`lines 35–39`), `up_cl = cloud * cloud_albedo(tau) * (up - up_wv)` is **100% absorbed by the ground (`abs_surf = down - up + up_cl`)**, ignoring surface albedo `alpha` on the second bounce. Over cloudy fresh snow (`alpha = 0.80, cloud = 1, tau = 10, cosZ = 0.5`), the code reports `absSurf = 108.96 W/m²` vs the exact multiple-bounce geometric series `(1 - alpha) / (1 - alpha * R_c * t_up) * down = 60.71 W/m²` — **`+79.5%` excess solar absorption on snowpack and sea ice**, severely biasing polar snow/ice albedo feedback.

2. **Split-model exported energy-budget non-closure (`+165.3 W/m²` = `11.9%` TOA violation) & sub-freezing liquid ocean (`SST = -8.50 °C` across `408 / 1,680` ocean cells) (`rust/boreal-sim/src/planet_fundamentals/energy/energy_update.rs:36–55`)**:
   - `energy_update.rs:36–42` drives surface temperature `TS` with Lacis–Hansen `column_budget::column(...)`, yet lines `48–50` compute exported `SWDOWN`, `QABS`, and `QREFL` from a **completely different radiative model** (`solar_budget::budget`: Meinel + Kasten–Czeplak) while exporting `REFLSW` and `ABSATM` from `column_budget`. On a sunlit cloudy ocean cell (`lat = -12.5°, cloud = 0.76`), `QABS + REFLSW + ABSATM = 1,556.5 W/m²` vs `TOA = 1,391.2 W/m²` (**`+165.3 W/m²` = `11.9%` TOA non-closure**).
   - Meanwhile, `energy_update.rs:42–45` integrates ocean `SST` without seawater freezing latent-heat buffering at `-1.8 °C` (UNESCO EOS-80 / Millero 1978, `35 PSU`), driving **`408 / 1,680` (`24.3%`)** of ocean cells below `-1.8 °C` down to **`-8.50 °C`** within 48 hours of January spin-up.

3. **`10.5 km`-tall supersaturated winter/polar ice-cloud monolith (`34 / 45` layers `>= 80%` RH to `11 km`, `topK = 190.0 K = -83.1 °C`, `tau = 28.0`), `tau = 150.0` hyper-arid desert convective towers with `RH = 15.7%` at LCL, and plateau/stratiform/ice radiative errors (`src/core/atmo/cloudColumn.ts:74–95, 146–160, 188–208`, `src/world/globe/localClouds.ts:105–257`)**:
   - In `cloudColumn.ts:77`, environmental humidity decays with a fixed `2.0 km` scale height (`q = q0 * exp(-z / 2000)`) while sub-zero `esatPa(tEnv)` decays faster (`~1.5–1.8 km` e-folding at `-10 °C` to `-25 °C`). Consequently, in winter air (`-10 °C, RH 77%, cover 0.8`), `RH` saturates to the `1.20` clamp across **`34 / 45` layers (`75.6%` of the troposphere, up to `11.0 km`, `-83.1 °C`, `tau = 28.0`)**, filling `54.2%` of the entire `32×32×12 km` `localClouds` 3D volume (`76.6%` of all voxels above `5 km`!) into a stepped winter voxel wall (`frame-0-winter-monolith-cloud.png`: `lcExt = 0.1177`, `whiteCloudFrac = 0.4518`, `brightFrac = 0.9221` vs thin low winter stratus in `ref-0-winter-arctic-stratus-sky.jpg`).
   - Conversely, in hyper-arid desert air (`35 °C, RH 15%, cover 0.1`), `cloudColumn.ts:74–86` forces a `4 km`-deep dry-adiabatic `tEnv` with zero CIN, never scales `conv` by `cover`, and caps every layer at `1.0 g/kg`, producing a **`2,250 m`-deep `tau = 150.0` (`waterPath = 1.776 kg/m²`) severe convective tower** whose environmental `RH` at the `4,000 m` well-mixed LCL is **`0.157` (`15.7%` instead of `100%`)** due to the contradictory boundary-layer `q` decay (`frame-1-sahara-desert-supercell.png`: flanking vertical white voxel towers & glowing dome, `whiteCloudFrac = 0.3498`, `brightFrac = 0.8330` vs clear cobalt sky in `ref-1-sahara-clear-dry-sky.jpg`).
   - Over high plateaus (`Tibet 4,500 m`), `cloudColumn.ts:76` integrates `11 km` AGL (`15,500 m` ASL = `4.5 km` above the `11 km` tropopause with no stratospheric inversion), `atmosphericCloudRadiativeEffect:207` divides by sea-level `p0_Pa = 101,325 Pa` instead of `pressureAt(4500) = 59,300 Pa` (`0.464` vs `0.793 K/day`, **`-41.5%` error**), `cloudBaseLongwave:146` returns **`0.0 W/m²`** whenever `lcl < 0` even for thick stratiform decks (`topLW = 73.9 W/m²`), `cloudTopLongwave:159` applies liquid downward `a0 = 0.130 m²/g` to glaciated ice tops (Ebert & Curry 1992 ice `~0.055 m²/g`, Stephens 1978 upward `0.158 m²/g`), and `surfaceCloudRadiativeEffect:195` uses un-attenuated TOA `K.TSI * mu`.
