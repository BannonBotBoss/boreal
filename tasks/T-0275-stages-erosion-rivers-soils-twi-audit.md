## Plain-English summary
Live headless Chromium inspection (`origin/main` `2b7eb8a`, `1280×720`, SwiftShader, `0` console/page errors) and calibrated Rule 11 verification across Boreal's staged terrain generation, Priority-Flood hydrology, curved river planform & breach solver, and Stage-3 surficial soils (`src/core/stages/elevation.ts`, `src/core/stages/flow.ts`, `src/core/stages/rivers.ts`, `src/core/stages/riverPlanform.ts`, `src/core/stages/riverContain.ts`, `src/core/stages/soils.ts`, `src/core/world.ts`) uncovered four coupled geomorphology and hydrology defects — with zero overlap with `T-0246` (home base-water sheet disappearance, underwater camera submersion optics, steep-reach boulder placement `channelRough.ts`, and remote inland lake detection), `T-0266` (grass blades/snowpack/loading), or `T-0272` (`tile.ts` globe shader/DEM/openTiles/`lakeMask`):

1. **Inverted gravity in Beyer/Lague droplet hydraulic erosion (`src/core/stages/elevation.ts:90–95`)**:
   - Line `90` defines `const dh = gh - h0`, so **`dh < 0` when flowing downhill** and `dh > 0` when flowing uphill (confirmed by `-dh` in `cap = Math.max(-dh * speed * water * capacity, minSlope)` on line `90`, uphill check `dh > 0` on line `92`, and `-dh` erosion bound on line `93`).
   - Yet line `95` updates droplet velocity with **`speed = Math.sqrt(Math.max(0, speed * speed + dh * gravity))`** (`+ dh * gravity` instead of `- dh * gravity`, inverting gravity `v² = v₀² − 2gΔh` from Beyer 2015 / Lague's `Erosion.cs`).
   - Over the `900,000` droplet steps on seed `1400`: **`57.45%` (`348,880 / 607,276`) of downhill steps decelerate** (vs `0.00%` physically), **`37.40%` (`336,600 / 900,000`) stall dead at `speed = 0`** (vs `0.22%` physically), **`292,640` uphill steps accelerate** droplets uphill (vs `0` physically), mean droplet speed collapses **`5.08×` (`0.3929` vs `1.9959`)**, and total hydraulic sediment incision drops **`56.3%` (`19,724.9` vs `45,134.1` grid units; RMS terrain delta `0.2895` vs `0.5099`)**.

2. **Vertical `3.82 m` river staircase cliffs, flat-crushed steep shelves, `-4.35 m` sub-lake plunging river mouths, false-pass midpoint containment, and `2.67×` double-counted `planformReport` (`src/core/stages/rivers.ts:162–167, 207–222`, `src/core/stages/flow.ts:27`, `src/core/stages/riverPlanform.ts:413–414, 487–490, 538–542`, `src/core/stages/riverContain.ts:22–43`)**:
   - **`breachUnconfined` staircase cliffs & flat-crushed reaches (`rivers.ts:164, 166, 207–222`)**: sets a single flat constant elevation ceiling `R.cap[geo.up]` from a single midpoint sample (`plan.samples`), clobbering **`85 / 181`** `up` cell IDs shared across line, main-stem fillet, and tributary join pieces. Clamping `surface = Math.min(filledS, cap)` crushes the entire upstream half of steep reaches (`slope = 0.090–0.141`) flat while leaving `hitFrom()` reporting steep `slope` and `speed = 0.91–0.96 m/s`, carving **`57 / 206` (`27.7%`) river junctions into vertical `0.50–3.818 m` shear box-canyon walls** (`-3.818 m` over `0.5 m` at `(2010, 3930)`, visible live as a giant rectangular dead-end excavated trench in `frame-0-river-cliff-staircase-2010-3930.png` and `recording.webm`; back-to-back `-3.457 m` and `-3.265 m` vertical cliffs at `(4294.5, 4140)` and `(4323.2, 4143.2)` in `frame-1-river-double-cliff-4295-4140.png`) plus `3` internal uphill arc steps.
   - **Sub-lake-level plunging river mouths (`flow.ts:27`, `riverPlanform.ts:413`)**: Priority-Flood seeds open-water `outlet` cells with submerged lake-floor bathymetry `filled[c] = h[c]` (down to `-50.74 m`) instead of `waterLevel` (`-0.25 m`). Consequently, **`16 / 60` (`26.7%`)** river mouths jump `> 0.15 m` from their receiving water body, and open-lake mouths dive up to **`4.10 m` BELOW Lake of the Woods** (`surf = -4.347 m` vs lake `-0.250 m` at `(6870, 1860)` in `frame-2-plunging-river-mouth-6870-1860.png`).
   - **False-pass `riverContainment` & `2.67×` double-counted `planformReport`**: `riverContain.ts` checks only the 1 midpoint per piece that `breachUnconfined` just capped, reporting `openShare = 0.000`, while dense `3 m` sampling along all `266` pieces finds **`114 / 2,823` (`4.04%`) unconfined water points floating up to `+0.386 m` above dry banks**. Meanwhile, `planformReport` (`riverPlanform.ts:541`) deduplicates rasterized carrier cells using cell-local offsets `(data[o], data[o+1])` that differ in every cell, inflating `meanderPieces` **`2.67×` (`104` reported vs `39` unique)** while **`51 / 338` (`15.1%`)** active planform cells saturate `SLOTS = 3`.

3. **`100%` of Stage-2c river-pond area (`5,374` cells = `483.7 ha` = `8.2%` of the map, `52` bodies up to `102.6 ha` & `11.96 m` deep) misclassified as dry `peat`/`clay` land in `soils.ts` (`src/core/stages/soils.ts:62, 69, 83, 103–106`)**:
   - `soilGrid` tests only `f.outlet[c] || L.id[c] >= 0`, completely omitting `riverPondGrid(seed).id[c] >= 0`. All **`5,374 / 5,374` (`100.0%`)** river-pond cells are treated as dry `land[]`, inflating land count by **`+12.69%` (`47,717` vs `42,343` true dry cells)**, pushing true dry-land `bedrock` outcrop exposure from the ESA WorldCover 2021 target `11.40%` up to **`12.85%`**, skewing `TPI`/`HAND`/`slope` z-scores (`sT, sH, sS`), and omitting river-pond shorelines from `HAND` drainage (`drain(c)`).
   - Every direct consumer of `soilAt()` (`ground.ts:168`, `continuousTerrain.ts:155`, `trees.ts:34`, `geometry.ts:87`, `world.ts:42`, `site.ts:66`) reads dry `peat`/`clay` inside 12-metre-deep lakes (e.g. `soilAt(1400, 3030, 2760).kind === 'peat'` on `102.6 ha`, `11.96 m`-deep pond `#16` in `frame-3-river-pond-shoreline-soil-mismatch.png`), producing **`5,991 / 23,267` (`25.7%`)** water half-cells classified as dry soil and **`543`** dry shoreline half-cells classified as `'water'` via `30 m` nearest-node snapping.

4. **`8.05×` unscaled raw-grid slope in `flow.ts:twi()` (`95.0%` of cells wrong by `> 1.0`) and `8.05×` unscaled `'fill depth (m)'` in `world.ts:38` (`src/core/stages/flow.ts:42–46`, `src/core/world.ts:38`)**:
   - `flow.ts:44` computes `s = Math.abs(f.filled[c] - f.filled[m]) / f.cell` on raw stage-1 grid units (missing `g.k = 8.0477 m/unit`), inflating TWI by `+ln(8.05) ≈ +2.085` (`meanDiff = 1.930`, `maxDiff = 5.459`, **`62,273 / 65,536` = `95.0%`** of all grid cells off by `> 1.0` vs world-scaled `soils.ts:S.twi`).
   - `world.ts:38` logs raw unscaled `fl.filled[c] - g.h[c]` labeled `'fill depth (m)'` (**`8.05×` too small**: `1.89 m` reported for a `15.18 m` depression fill) alongside both `twi(fl, c)` and `so.twi` in the Generation Inspector, displaying two contradictory TWI values differing by `~2.0` for the exact same cell.
