1. **Animal Route-Plan `4.00×` Mechanical-vs-Metabolic Jump Under-Pricing, `0.00 m` Rock Margin, Oblique Log-Jump Crash, Multi-Blocker Detour Blindness & Remote Tundra `scaleMul` Bypass (`src/core/{plan.ts:104–173, world.ts:88–93, sitePhase.ts:12–62, siteHeight.ts:8–11}`, `src/render/pictureGate.ts:8–13`)**:
   - `jumpJPerKg()` (`plan.ts:104–107`) computes **pure mechanical** vertical launch kinetic energy (`0.5 v₀² = 8.71 J/kg`) and compares it directly (`plan.ts:167`) against **metabolic** detour energy (`travelJ(m, v, extra)/m` from Taylor, Heglund & Maloiy 1982, *JEB* 97:1), omitting mammalian skeletal-muscle efficiency (`η ≈ 0.25`, Heglund et al. 1982, *JEB* 97:57; Alexander 2003). Jumping is under-priced **`4.00×` (`-75.0%`)**, making a 38 kg wolf at `3.0 m/s` leap over a `0.89 m` ballistic arc whenever a detour exceeds just **`2.15 m`** instead of the true metabolic break-even **`8.59 m`**.
   - `blocksFrom()` (`plan.ts:125`) sets rock clearance `r = rockDiameter(e) / 2` with **`0.00 m` body margin** (omitting `BODY_CLEAR_M = 0.20 m` applied to logs at line 123), so routed wolves clip `0.20 m` into Shield boulders, while spruce/person use fixed `BLOCK.wolf.trunk = 0.70 m` ignoring `e.scale`.
   - At a **`30°` oblique approach** to a log (`r = 0.63 m`, `speed = 2.0 m/s`, `hopM = 1.73 m`), the crossing chord along the animal's path is **`2.52 m`** (`1.45×` longer than `hopM`), yet `plan.ts:166–169` assumes a perpendicular (`90°`) crossing (`2r = 1.26 m`) and commands **`motion = 'jump'`**, landing the wolf `0.79 m` short directly inside the log.
   - `ends(hit)` (`plan.ts:156–162`) never checks whether the detour path `from -> to -> goal` intersects other blockers in `blocks`: when trees stand at log ends, `plan()` routes straight into an adjacent tree (`detourDistToTree = 0.00 m < 0.70 m`).
   - In `world.ts:88–93`, when `SITE.remote === true`, `fit` is forced to `null` (`SITE` in `sitePhase.ts` has no `scaleMul` field), skipping `scaleMul` on all off-home landings: with `scaleMul = 0.125` (stunted subarctic spruce), home trees cap at `scale = 0.17` while remote tundra trees spawn at **`scale = 1.29`** (**`7.6×` too tall**!). `remoteGameHeight(x, z)` (`siteHeight.ts:9–11`) passes `NaN` through unguarded into `heightAt()`.
