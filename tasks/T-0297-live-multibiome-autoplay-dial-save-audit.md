# Fix remote multi-biome ground landings (floating `+2.7 m` grass wall in Pacific sky, Kenora staged lake/wading ripples & `drink` prompt in dry Sahara/Greenland/saltwater Pacific, boreal pups/hares/grass everywhere), live `?autoplay=1` `>57.8 s` hang on `#loading` `"Ready"` behind `inpage.prewarm()`, ground Time Dial `±1×` hold-detent lock & silent rewind HUD, and `Esc -> Save -> Continue` `5.92 m` / `156.5°` reset

Status: proposed, unclaimed. Needs another agent's `approve-plan`. Credits = `10 × 4 objectives × (1 + 0.3 research) = 52`.

## Plain-English summary
- Landing remotely from the in-page globe onto **three radically different Earth environments** — **Central Sahara Desert (`23.5°N, 10.0°E`)**, **Central Greenland Ice Sheet (`72.5°N, -40.0°W`)**, and the **Open Equatorial Pacific Ocean (`0.0°N, -140.0°W`)** — breaks completely on the ground:
  - Moving from Greenland (`+2.5 m`) to the **Pacific Ocean (`-0.2 m`)** leaves cached grass tiles hanging **`+2.7 m` in mid-air**, filling **`59.6%`** of the upper Pacific sky with a literal upside-down wall of green grass (`frame-2`), while the wolf water-skis across open ocean with `5` wolf pups, `5` snowshoe hares, and **`E / click · drink`** on `35 PSU` saltwater.
  - Because `waterAt()` (`src/core/needs/water.ts:11`) checks Kenora's staged lake (`lakeAt(STAGES.seed, 0, 0)`) without checking `!SITE.remote`, **both dry Sahara Desert (`frame-0`) and Greenland Ice Sheet (`frame-1`)** project glowing cyan wading ripples right through the wolf's belly and display **`E / click · drink`**, while rendering lush green grass (`32.8%` dark green in Sahara; **`84.7%` dark green / `0.00%` snow-ice** on the `3,000 m` Greenland Ice Sheet, HUD `Snow 0.0 cm`), boreal spruce trees, `5` wolf pups, and `5` snowshoe hares everywhere on Earth.
- Running Matthew's documented self-playing test **`/?autoplay=1`** (`AGENTS.md`) hangs past **`57.8 seconds`** permanently stuck on `#loading` reading **`"Ready"`** (`100%` gold progress bar, `frame-3`) at `AUTOPLAY step 1: waiting through loading t=0.0s` (`autoplayDone = false`) — because `main.ts:815` runs `await inpage?.prewarm()` *after* `startup()` sets `"Ready"`, `startView('?autoplay=1')` returns `'ground'` (dropping `stages=1&trees=v2`), and `autoplay.ts:110` disables `stuck(run)` whenever `#loading` is present.
- Holding `,` (rewind Time Dial) continuously for **`2.2 s`** on the ground gets permanently trapped at **`dial = -0.01` (`-1.000×`)** instead of accelerating to `-100×` rewind because `main.ts:601–602` trips `worldClock.ts:267`'s `detentDir` on frame 1 and locks every subsequent held frame; `#status` never displays active clock rate or `REWIND`, so after `Continue` the world silently runs backwards (`08:06 -> 08:01`) with zero indicator; and `loadClock()` blindly adds `+60 s` forward `offlineSec` even when saved paused (`dial = 0`) or rewinding (`rate = -1×`).
- Walking **`5.92 m`** away from spawn, opening Pause (`Esc`), clicking **`Save`** (`Saved 6:48:35 PM`), reloading, and clicking **`Continue`** teleports the player **`5.92 m`** back to spawn with a **`156.5°` (`2.731 rad`)** heading jump (`frame-4`), because `captureResume()` (`src/main.ts:516`) silently bails whenever `!hasSeenBigBang(context)` — and `ResumePlace` never stores remote landing `SITE` coordinates either.
- What stays the same: all keyboard, mouse, and gamepad bindings, Rust simulation ownership (`AGENTS.md`), and the unit-tested Kenora home ecosystem.

2. **Live `/?autoplay=1` Self-Playing Test Hangs `>57.8 s` Stuck on `#loading` `"Ready"` Behind `inpage.prewarm()`, Drops `stages=1&trees=v2`, and Disables Stall Detection While Loading (`frame-3-autoplay-visit.png`, `src/main.ts:804–828`, `src/world/globe/startView.ts:11–19`, `src/world/globe/inpage.ts:512–529`, `src/input/autoplay.ts:110,138,254,274,291–298`)**:
   - Loading exact `/?autoplay=1` on `origin/main` (`c4209c0b`) remained stuck on `#loading` for the entire **`57.8 s`** window (`stillLoadingAtEnd = true`, `stepCount = 1`: `"AUTOPLAY step 1: waiting through loading t=0.0s"`, `done = false`), even though `startup()` had already set the progress bar to **`"Ready"` (`100%`) at `t = 51.9 s`** and the game clock had been ticking behind `#loading` since `t = 14.8 s` (`08:02 -> 08:10`).
   - **Root causes**:
     1. `startup()` (`startup.ts:242`) prints `"Ready"` (`100%`) *before* `main.ts:815` runs `await inpage?.prewarm()`, which then dynamically imports `globeView.ts`, compiles all globe shaders, runs `forestLanding()`, and loops up to `5,000 ms` with no overall timeout while `#loading` lies that it is `"Ready"`.
     2. `startView('?autoplay=1')` (`startView.ts:15–16`) sees `[...q.keys()].length === 1` (`autoplay`), returns `'ground'` instead of `'sphere'`, and `bareStart('?autoplay=1')` returns `null` (skipping `stages=1&trees=v2`), setting `cine = false` (`main.ts:804`) and forcing the slow ground Ultra probe + `inpage.prewarm()` path.

3. **Ground Time Dial (`hold ,` / `hold .` / `;`) Permanently Traps Continuous Rewind Hold at the `-1×` Detent, Hides Active Rewind/Rate on `#status`, and Credits Forward `offlineSec` to Paused/Rewinding Saves (`src/core/worldClock.ts:163–167,249–276`, `src/main.ts:598–605,797`)**:
   - **Detent trap (`worldClock.ts:263–275` + `main.ts:601–602`)**: Starting from `+1×` (`dial = +0.01`), holding `,` (`timeSlower`) runs `nudgeDial(-0.08 * dt)` followed on frame 1 by `nudgeDial(-0.02)`, crossing `-0.01` and latching `detentDir = -1`. Every subsequent frame of the continuous hold hits `if (detentDir !== 0 && Math.sign(delta) === detentDir) return S.dial;` (`worldClock.ts:267`). Both live Edge capture (`savedClock.dial = -0.01, rate = -1.000`) and deterministic 60 Hz simulation (`2.2 s` hold -> `dial = -0.0100, rate = -1.0000×` vs expected `dial = -0.1860`, `rate <= -50×`) prove a continuous rewind hold can **never** move faster than `-1×` until released and pressed a second time!
   - **Silent rewind on `#status` (`main.ts:797`)**: `#status` prints `PAUSED · ` only when `isPaused()`, and never prints `formatRate(clockRate())` or `REWIND` when `clockRate() < 0` or `|clockRate() - 1| > 0.05`. After `Continue`, the live game ran backwards (`08:06 -> 08:01`) with `#status` reading `"08:01 · WALK · ..."` — indistinguishable from forward `+1×`.
   - **`loadClock()` offline credit bug (`worldClock.ts:165–166`)**: Calling `loadClock()` `60 s` after saving while paused (`dial = 0`) or rewinding (`dial = -0.01, rate = -1×`) blindly credits **`offlineSec = +60 s`** forward to `S.baseSec`!

4. **`Esc -> Save` -> Reload -> `Continue` Discards Ground Position & Heading (`5.92 m` / `156.5°` Reset) and Never Persists Remote Landing `SITE` (`frame-4-time-dial-save-continue.png`, `src/main.ts:305–315,515–528`)**:
