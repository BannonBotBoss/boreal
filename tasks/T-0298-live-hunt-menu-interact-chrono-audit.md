## Plain-English summary
- Pinning and carrying a snowshoe hare on staged terrain buries the carried hare **`1.85 m` underground** (`hare.y = 0.56 m` while the wolf stands at `wolf.y = 2.40 m`, `frame-0`) because `carryPlace()` never adds `heightAt(wolf.x, wolf.z)`, and rotates the hare with Three.js's default **`'XYZ'`** Euler order instead of **`'YXZ'`** (`71.68°` roll error and `0.203 m` nape detachment at `yaw = 1.2 rad`), while `main.ts:776` hardcodes `"Pinned. X eats, B releases."` ignoring custom rebindings.
- Opening **`Pause -> More`** sub-panels breaks three live user flows:
  - Editing any slider in **`More -> Appearance`** changes **`0.00%`** of the 3D canvas (`379,478 B === 379,478 B` byte-identical PNG before & after moving 6 sliders, `frame-1`) because `main.ts:757` suppresses `drawFrame()` while `menu.open` is true.
  - Clicking **`More -> Planet view (Shift+G)`** sets `body.on-globe` (hiding the HUD) without calling `menu.close()`, trapping the player on an empty **`"Planet view (Shift+G)\nBack"`** modal over a permanently frozen forest frame (`frame-2`) because `main.ts:757` blocks `inpage.frame()` while `menu.open` is true!
  - Clicking **`More -> Photo mode (P)`** (or `Generation inspector (I)`) runs `menu.close()` inside `p.build()` *before* `menu.ts:66`'s `page(...)` runs, building an orphan `"Photo mode (P)\nBack"` screen and stealing keyboard focus onto the hidden fading `Back` button.
- Live ground interactions (`E` snout-push rock & gather herb) have three broken mechanics:
  - **Literally `0 / 56` (`0.0%`) of forest-floor rocks (`s.rock < 0.2`)** on staged terrain can ever be pushed, because `placeRockSeed()` (`rocks.ts:69–70`) rejects every rock with `rockHeight <= 0.22 m` while `pushContact()` (`rocks.ts:78`) rejects every rock with `rockDiameter > 0.22 m` — two mathematically disjoint sets!
  - Gathering a practice herb prints **`"Herb gathered. Its removal is remembered."`** (`frame-3`), yet writes `removed: false` and leaves the herb standing & glowing; `1.5 s` later (`0.00000184 g = 0.00018%` regrowth > `1e-6`), `events.ts:46,64–65` re-enables `E / click · gather` and awards another **full `+1` herb** for `1.84 µg` of regrowth!
  - Every `E` interaction increments `revision++` (`main.ts:206`), forcing `buildScene.ts:237` to rebuild **every single loaded chunk** through terrain workers (`~0.5–1.5 s` stall), starving `pushFrom` roll animation while old chunks remain mounted.
- Opening the **Chronometer (`T`, `frame-4`)** never displays the calendar date even when scrubbing crosses midnight (`dayAt` advances `+1` day), leaves `weatherAt().tC` frozen across a 12-hour noon-to-midnight scrub (`18.86 °C -> 18.69 °C`, only `-0.17 °C`), and wipes held scrub arrow keys via `main.ts:416` `clearInput()` (`12.09 -> 12.10` over a `2.2 s` `ArrowRight` hold); meanwhile `#help-binds` (`main.ts:506`) still claims `"P photo mode (free camera, rewind, capture)"` even though pressing `P` leaves `#photo.hidden === true`, and exiting Photo Mode into `#photo-gallery` leaves `body.photo-mode` active throughout Gallery review.
- What stays the same: all existing keyboard, mouse, and gamepad bindings, Rust simulation ownership (`AGENTS.md`), and prior audit tasks `T-0001..T-0297` (zero overlap with `T-0227`, `T-0238`, `T-0239`, `T-0243`, `T-0257`, `T-0260`, `T-0273`, `T-0297`).
