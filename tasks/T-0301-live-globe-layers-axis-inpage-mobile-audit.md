## Plain-English summary
- On the **Planet Globe (`?view=globe`)** (`frame-0`, `recording.webm = 370,162 B`), **4 of the 11 checkboxes in the top-right Layer Panel do nothing at orbital altitude (`14,016 km`)**: unchecking **`Clouds`**, **`Vegetation`**, **`Ocean`**, or **`Venus`** produces **bit-identical planet frames** (`21,019` white cloud px, `8,222` blue ocean px, `0.5693` mean luma across all 4 — vs positive control `Sunlight` dropping white px to `18` and luma to `0.1370`, MAD `78.67`).
