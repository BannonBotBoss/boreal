# T-0143 the litter follows her

The Rust store can birth a gray wolf litter: five pups, the middle of the usual 4–6 (Mech & Boitani 2003), four weeks old so they can walk, parented to the mama, standing on a ring around the den. Each frame they walk toward the trail behind her. The page uses the same numbers. The meshes are the wolf skin, scaled down, without the adult fur cards.

`litter.png` is the emulator after the drop, once the nearest pup had walked to about 6 m. She was turned to face that pup so the still could see it. A small pale wolf stands beside her muzzle. The log in `litter.json` is all five: stage `pup`, parent the mama's handle, closing from about 34 m to about 6 m. The cyan rings are the emulator's hands.

This is software graphics, not a Quest. On this machine the frames are slow, so the walk uses the real time between frames.
