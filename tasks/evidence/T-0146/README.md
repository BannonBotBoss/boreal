# T-0146 pups grow on the world clock

Five pups are born at four weeks. The Rust litter rules advance their age from the world clock. Ten real minutes at the default clock (300 real seconds per game hour, so 7200 game seconds) is half a year. In that span they get larger, their steps get longer and slower, and they walk closer behind their mother. Turning the clock backward does not make them younger.

The frames use a faster dial so the same growth does not take a ten-minute sitting. The seconds-per-year number is the one in the test.

## Frames

Software graphics, 1280×720, ground view, hour started at 10:00.

- `young.png` — just after they are born. Age about 0.08 years, still pups. A row of small wolves is on the right, far from the mama.
- `grown-day.png` — the clock has run. Age about 0.89 years, juvenile. A line of larger wolves stands off to the right. The mama is still very bright against the night; that lighting is the day-and-night step, not this one.

`ages.json` and `day.json` are the ages read off the page.
