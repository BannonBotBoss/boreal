# T-0147 day and night from the clock

The forest hour is the world clock. `?hour=10` is morning. `?hour=22` is night. The same sky update runs in the flat view and in the VR forest.

The mama was a lamp after dark. Her stored coat is drawn with a strong fill so she still reads when the key light is dim, and that fill was not turned down at night, and it skipped tone mapping. At night the fill drops to a small fraction and tone mapping comes back, so she sits in the moonlight with the trees.
