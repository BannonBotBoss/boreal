The share is tan pixels (red above green above blue) below luma 90, over all such pixels in the lower 45% of the frame. A pale solid coat has almost none. The woolly coat has about a third.

Mech and Boitani 2003, Wolves: Behavior, Ecology, and Conservation: pups emerge from the den woolly. The card count is a cover so the tube does not show through, not a hairs-per-square-centimetre count. The adult coat uses 18000 cards.
