## What was built / fixed
1. **Ground sky lunar date and site latitude propagation (`src/render/sky.ts`)**:
   - `createSky.update` already computed `doy = dayAt(time)` and `sun = sunPosition(hour, doy, lat)` (`lat = siteLatitude(VENUS_SITE.lat)`), yet line 141 still called `moonAge(WEATHER.day)` (stale whenever `WEATHER.on = false` or when `sky.update` is evaluated at an explicit `time`) and `moon = sunPosition(hour - age / SYNODIC * 24, doy)` (hardcoded default `LATITUDE = 49.5°N` instead of `lat`).
   - Wired both calls to `age = moonAge(doy)` and `moon = sunPosition(hour - age / SYNODIC * 24, doy, lat)` so the sun and moon share the exact authoritative simulation date and landed observer latitude.
2. **Planet-frame solar direction latitude alignment (`src/world/globe/planet.ts`, `src/world/globe/globeView.ts`, `src/render/trees/overheadCrowns.ts`)**:
   - `globeView.ts` (`sunNow`) and `overheadCrowns.ts` (`shadowAway`) called `sunPosition(hourAt(sec), dayAt(sec))` with 2 arguments (`LATITUDE = 49.5°N` default) and then rotated `(d.x, d.y, -d.z)` through `(planet.east, planet.home, planet.north)` centered at `PLANET.home.latDeg = 49.77°N`, shifting the planet's geocentric subsolar latitude northward by `+0.2700°` (`~30 km`) at every hour and season.
   - Added pure helper `planetSunDirection(planet, hour, dayOfYear)` in `src/world/globe/planet.ts` evaluating `sunPosition` at `toLatLon(planet.home).lat * 180 / Math.PI` and routed both `globeView.ts` and `overheadCrowns.ts` through it, reducing geocentric subsolar latitude error vs `declination(day)` from `0.2700°` to `< 1e-9°`.
3. **Automated NOAA verification & reusable skill (`tests/seasonal-sky.test.ts`, `notes/skills/boreal-seasonal-light-audit.md`, `AGENT-NOTES.md`)**:
   - Added `tests/seasonal-sky.test.ts` testing `day 227` baseline preservation, `day 355` winter solstice noon geometry, positive multi-year (`366, 592, 1085`) and negative rewind (`0, -10`) rollover periodicity, `createSky` sun/moon date & Southern Hemisphere landing site propagation, and `planetSunDirection` + `shadowAway` geocentric declination exactness against NOAA Spencer (1971).
