## 1. What I Built

1. **Littoral Site & Depth/Slope Niche (`src/core/flora/site.ts`):**
   - Extended `Site` with `waterDepth` (`m` below local water surface, `0` on dry land), `waterSurface` (`m` world elevation of local water surface), and `flow` (`0..1` river channel order/flow).
   - Extended `Niche` with `waterDepth?: [number, number]` (`m` range where littoral emergents/floating plants grow; undefined = terrestrial only, `waterDepth === 0`) and `maxSlopeDeg?: number` (steep wave-washed shore cutoff, Duarte & Kalff 1986 *Limnol. Oceanogr.* 31:1072–1080).
   - In `siteAt(x, z)`, shallow base-water lake margins and low-order stream margins (`waterDepth <= 1.6 m`, `!lake.active`, `river.flow <= 0.12`) retain their organic/mineral sediment depth (`soil > 0`) and wetness (`1.0`) so littoral macrophytes can root in the submerged bed, while perched kettle pits (`lake.active`), deep open water (`waterDepth > 1.6 m`), and fast river channels remain excluded.
   - `suitability(n, s)` returns `0` for terrestrial species when `s.water` is true and `n.waterDepth` is undefined, and evaluates a smooth edge taper across `n.waterDepth` and `n.maxSlopeDeg` for littoral species.

2. **Littoral Emergent Cover Rows & Submerged Culm Elongation (`src/render/grass/cover.ts`, `src/render/grass/grass.ts`):**
   - Extended `CoverKind` with `depth?: [number, number]` and `maxSlope?: number`.
   - Updated `sedge` (*Carex aquatilis / C. utriculata*) with `depth: [0, 0.55]` and `maxSlope: 0.25` (Vitt 1994; Flora of North America Vol. 23).
   - Added data rows for `horsetail` (*Equisetum fluviatile*, `h: 0.65 m`, `0.38–1.05 m`, `w: 3.5 mm`, `depth: [0, 0.95]`, `maxSlope: 0.22`, `standScale: 8`, FNA Vol. 2; Mäkelä et al. 2004) and `bulrush` (*Schoenoplectus tabernaemontani*, `h: 1.15 m`, `0.75–1.65 m`, `w: 4.8 mm`, `depth: [0.05, 1.15]`, `maxSlope: 0.20`, `standScale: 10`, FNA Vol. 23; Mäkelä et al. 2004).
   - In `fillTile` (`src/render/grass/grass.ts`), emergent culms stay rooted on the submerged lake bed (`root.y = h`) and elongate through the water column (`Math.max(waterDepth + 0.18, hScale + waterDepth * 0.92)`, Grace 1989 *Func. Ecol.* 3:593) so aerial tips stand above `LEVEL.waterSurface`.
   - In the grass vertex shader, clamped the shoreline wet-darkening factor with `step(-0.22, root.y)` so submerged emergent stems keep their green chlorophyll colour instead of turning dark mud brown.

3. **3D Littoral Flora Rows & Horizontal Floating Lily Pads (`src/core/flora/plants.ts`, `src/render/flora/plant.ts`, `src/render/flora/flora.ts`):**
   - Added `floating?: boolean` to `FloraSpec` and added three littoral species rows to `FLORA`:
     - `horsetail` (*Equisetum fluviatile*): `0.38–1.05 m` (`mean 0.65 m`), `whorled` linear branchlets (`8` per node across `7` nodes, ascending `droop: -0.35`), `waterDepth: [0, 0.95]`, `maxSlopeDeg: 13`.
     - `bulrush` (*Schoenoplectus tabernaemontani*): `0.75–1.65 m` (`mean 1.15 m`), `graminoid`, compact nodding brown terminal spikelet cluster (`flower: { kind: 'solitary', nod: 0.45 }`), `waterDepth: [0.05, 1.15]`, `maxSlopeDeg: 12`.
     - `pond-lily` (*Nuphar variegata*): `floating: true`, `basal` rosette of `5` `orbicular` floating leaves (`0.20 × 0.17 m`, FNA Vol. 3) plus a solitary `4.2 cm` yellow cup flower (`#e6b81c`, `6` cupped sepals, DOY `[150, 235]`), `waterDepth: [0.22, 1.55]`, `maxSlopeDeg: 11`.
   - Added `'horsetail'`, `'bulrush'`, and `'pond-lily'` to `CARPET_IDS` (`src/render/flora/flora.ts`) so near-shore rhizomatous stands batch into far carpet clumps seamlessly, and anchored `p.floating` plants at `y = site.waterSurface + 0.012 m`.
   - In `buildPlant` (`src/render/flora/plant.ts`), oriented `spec.floating` leaf blades with `frame(padAt, dir, -Math.PI / 2 - az)` so local `X` (pad width) aligns with the horizontal rotation axis `UP × dir` and local `Z` (pad normal) points straight up (`|normal.y| = 0.9998`).
