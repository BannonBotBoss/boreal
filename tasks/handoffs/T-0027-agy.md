## What was built
1. **Reused botanical generator (`src/render/geometry.ts`, `src/render/batch.ts`)**:
   - Replaced the two placeholder icosahedron loops (`entity.kind === 'forage'` and `entity.kind === 'plant'`) in `src/render/geometry.ts` with `propFlora(kind, seed, detail)` backed by `buildClump` / `buildPlant` (`src/render/flora/plant.ts`) and sourced species rows in `FLORA_BY_ID` (`src/core/flora/plants.ts`).
   - `forage` (`Forage berry patch`): uses `FLORA_BY_ID.blueberry` (*Vaccinium angustifolium*) with a clonal mat (`ramets: 12`, `radius: 0.38 m`, `stems: 2`, `branching: 3`) and terminal corymbs of `9 mm` blue berries (`petalLength: 0.009 m`, `petalWidth: 0.007 m`, `open: 0.35`, `nod: 2.2`, `cup: 0.003`, `#4d6bb3`). Across 122 generated `forage` entities in chunks `(0,0)..(2,2)`, total shrub height is `0.199–0.331 m` (median `0.260 m`) and patch radius is `0.273–0.457 m` (median `0.354 m`).
   - `plant` (`Practice herb`): deterministically selects among 4 low boreal understorey herbs in `FLORA_BY_ID` (`bunchberry` *Cornus canadensis*, `bluebead` *Clintonia borealis*, `woodlily` *Lilium philadelphicum*, `everlasting` *Anaphalis margaritacea*; excluding tall `fireweed` per Grok's plan review note). Across generated `plant` entities in chunks `(0,0)..(2,2)`, height is `0.189–0.515 m` (median `0.359 m`, `0.25–0.69x` wolf withers height `0.75 m`).
   - Stripped `aFall`, `aOrgan`, `aBloom` attributes and normalized any degenerate tube-seam vertex normals in `stripFlora(g)` so all prop geometries carry `['color', 'normal', 'position']` with unit normals, cached per `(kind, species, variant, detail)` on shared `PROP_MATERIAL` (`side: THREE.DoubleSide`), adding **zero extra draw calls or shader programs**.
2. **Added unit test (`tests/library.test.ts`)**:
   - Added `'botanical forage and plant props stay below wolf withers (0.55 m) and use shared PROP_MATERIAL geometry'` verifying attribute compatibility, unit normals (`tests/render-math.test.ts`), and height bounds (`0.12–0.55 m`).
