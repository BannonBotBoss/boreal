## What Was Built
1. **Atomic Chunk-Commit Registration for Deer Rigs (`src/render/buildScene.ts`)**:
   - In `createChunk(cx, cz, context, near)`, prepared deer rigs (`catalog[entity.kind].objectType === 'animal'`) are collected into a chunk-local `deer: { rig, entity }[]` list instead of calling `scene.add(rig.root)` and `animals.set(entity.key, { rig, entity })` mid-generator while `createChunk` is still yielding across frames.
   - Added exported helper `commitChunkAnimals(deer, animals, scene, dispose = disposeObject)`, invoked in `sync`'s `enqueue(..., done)` callback immediately after `chunks.set(key, c)`. If `previous = animals.get(entity.key)` already exists (from a chunk rebuild), `commitChunkAnimals` copies its live `x`, `z`, `rotation`, and `energy` onto the new entity, updates `previous.entity = entity`, and disposes the unused duplicate `rig.root`; otherwise it adds `rig.root` to `scene` and sets `animals.set(entity.key, { rig, entity })`.
   - Added exported helper `shouldRetainChunkAnimal(key, keys, chunkJobs)` (`keys.has(key) || chunkJobs.has(key.slice(0, key.lastIndexOf(':')))`) used by `simulateAnimals` so live animals are retained across in-flight chunk tier rebuilds (`near <-> mid`) and only pruned when their chunk is neither in `chunks` nor in `chunkJobs`.
2. **Unit & Integration Tests (`tests/deer-rig-commit.test.ts`)**:
   - Verifies `createChunk` does not call `animals.set` or `scene.add(rig.root)` mid-job.
   - Simulates a multi-slice chunk job (`enqueue` + `runJobs(0)`) interleaved with `simulateAnimals`'s prune check on seed `1400` chunk `(1, 0)` and verifies all three deer (`1:0:1` at `62.16, 7.44`, `1:0:15` at `49.65, 18.16`, `1:0:34` at `66.27, 30.91`) survive chunk commit and remain attached to `scene`.
   - Verifies in-flight chunk rebuilds preserve moved deer state (`x, z, rotation, energy`) and dispose duplicate prebuilt rigs, while unloaded chunks prune cleanly.
3. **Reusable Skill (`notes/skills/chunk-commit-animal-lifecycle.md`)**:
   - Documented the generator-prepare / `done`-commit pattern for streamed chunk entities and updated `tasks/T-0047-deer-rig-chunk-commit.md` header to `skill: chunk-commit-animal-lifecycle`.
