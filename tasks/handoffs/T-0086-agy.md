## What was built
1. **Cumulative precipitation deficit (`src/core/weather.ts`, `src/core/planetWeather.ts`, `rust/boreal-sim/src/planet_fundamentals/hydrosphere/soil_moisture.rs`)**:
   - `expectedPrecipMmDay(day)`: reads the existing Kenora A 1981–2010 monthly precipitation normal `NORMALS.precip` (`[27, 20, 29, 34, 76, 108, 101, 76, 89, 60, 42, 27]` mm/month, annual total `689 mm`) via `monthValue(NORMALS.precip, day) / MONTH_DAYS` (`30.44 d`). No second monthly table was added.
   - `stepMoistureDebt(debtMm, expectedRainMm, actualRainMm)` and `MoistureTracker`: accumulate `debtMm = Math.max(0, debtMm + expectedRainMm - actualRainMm)` in mm of water equivalent (`kg/m²`), matching Issue #100 (`MoistureDebt = ExpectedRainfall - ActualRainfall`) and Issue #101 (`MoistureTracker::update`). A dry spell (`actualRainMm < expectedRainMm`) raises the debt; a wet spell (`actualRainMm > expectedRainMm`) pays it down; a deluge clamps at `0` and never goes negative.
   - `moistureDebtIndex(debtMm)`: scales cumulative precipitation deficit by the Manabe (1969) root-zone bucket field capacity `HYDRO.bucketMm = 150 mm` onto `0..1`.
   - `grid.debt`: per-column `Float32Array(NX * NX)` on the near weather grid, updated in `kessler(dt)` and `catchUpWeather(dtSec)`, shifted on `recentre`, and saved/loaded in `saveWeather` / `loadWeather`.
   - `weatherAt(x, z)` and `moistureDebtAt(x, z)`: expose `debtMm` (`>= 0` mm) and `debt` (`0..1`) on `PointWeather` for the cell the player stands in, both when standing in the near cell and when reading the Rust planet grid (`accumulatePlanetMoistureDebt` in `src/core/planetWeather.ts`).
   - Rust crate (`rust/boreal-sim/src/planet_fundamentals/hydrosphere/soil_moisture.rs`): added `step_moisture_debt` and `MoistureTracker` with unit test `moisture_debt_rises_on_dry_spell_and_clamps_at_zero`.
2. **Tests & Skill**:
