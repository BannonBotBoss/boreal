   - Wired real ground speeds (`m/s`) for `hare` (`Math.abs(h.sim.speed)`), `deer` (`Math.abs(view.lookup(key)?.brain?.speed ?? 0)`), and `person` (`Math.abs(view.visitorOf(key)?.speed ?? 0)`).
   - Fixed hare enumeration in `src/ui/senses.ts` to filter by `h.sim.alive && !h.embodied` rather than `h.root.visible` (because `src/render/animalPicture.ts` `setBodyShare(row.root, row.proc)` sets `h.root.visible = false` whenever a hare's Meshy skin or mid-distance impostor is drawn, which previously hid skinned hares from both `smell()` and `see()`).
   - Kept non-visual olfactory trace deltas (`scent`, `droppings` from `deltaSources`) and stationary background flora (`plant`, `forage`, `log`) strictly in `sources` (`smell()`) while passing only physical animal/visitor bodies (`deer`, `hare`, `person`, excluding self `embodied`) and physical `carcass` deltas into `visualBodies` (`see()`).
   - Exposed `sights: () => lastSeen` alongside `sounds` and `scents`.
3. **Regression tests & reusable skill (`tests/sense-pictures.test.ts`, `notes/skills/sense-pictures.md`)**:
