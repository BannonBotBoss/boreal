## What Was Built

1. **Landed-site latitude propagation in circadian behaviour (`src/core/needs/behaviour.ts`, `src/main.ts`)**:
   - Defaulted `sunTimes(dayOfYear: number, latitude = siteLatitude(LATITUDE))` in `src/core/needs/behaviour.ts` to `siteLatitude(LATITUDE)` (`src/core/sitePhase.ts`).
   - Updated `src/main.ts` (`sunTimes(dayAt(wNow), siteLatitude(LATITUDE))`) so autonomous animal circadian windows (`activityAt`, `chooseGoal`) match the local solar sunrise and sunset at any landed globe latitude across all seasons.
2. **Seasonal & latitude-aware headless `SimWorld` predator schedule (`src/core/sim/index.ts`, `src/animals/hare.ts`)**:
   - Added `day: () => number` and `latitude: () => number` callbacks to `SimWorld` and `WorldOptions` (`src/core/sim/index.ts`, defaulting to `() => DAY_OF_YEAR` and `() => siteLatitude(LATITUDE)`), and replaced hardcoded `{ sunrise: 6, sunset: 18 }` in `stepPredator` with `sunTimes(w.day(), w.latitude())`.

3. **Unit tests (`tests/photoperiod-site.test.ts`)**:
   - Added `tests/photoperiod-site.test.ts` verifying `sunTimes`, `activityAt`, and `chooseGoal` at `49.5°N` and `64.5°N` on summer (`day = 172`) and winter (`day = 355`) solstices, `stepPredator` movement at `49.5°N` summer dawn (`hour = 4.2`) and rest at `64.5°N` winter night (`hour = 6.2`), and static propagation in `src/main.ts` and `src/animals/hare.ts`.
